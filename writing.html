1 写作绘图

本节课目标

完成本节课堂训练及课后练习后，学生应能：

  1. 从实验结果中识别核心发现，并按读者依次产生的问题组织论证顺序。
  2. 将论文中的数字、表格与图追溯至数据行、配置和统计口径，判断图文是否一致。
  3. 依据图所要回答的比较设计数据图，并在最终版面尺寸下检验其可读性和不确定性表达。
  4. 设计职责、输入范围和审阅版本明确的独立审阅，并区分审阅完成与审阅通过。
  5. 将有效的修订方法抽象为可复用规范，并在另一份研究材料上检验其迁移。

课堂训练前四项能力的初步运用；按意见返修和第五项的迁移检验在课后独立完成。

课前完成准备，课堂 60 分钟，课后完成复习、独立实践与迁移评价，另可选做深入专题。

课前准备 · 课堂教学 · 课后复习与扩展

本节以一篇关于标签噪声学习的四页练习论文为分析材料。实验中训练标签有四成错误，网络在训练早期已能对大部分样本作出正确预测；继续训练后，测试表现反而下降。练习稿把这一现象放在背景介绍和方法清单之后，读者读完摘要仍不清楚研究的贡献。课程借此讨论两个问题：怎样围绕核心发现重新组织论文，以及怎样核查文本、图表与数据的一致性。

材料中的实验已在本机完成，原始数据和运行脚本随材料提供。课堂直接使用现成数据，时间集中在论文表达与结果核查上。

课堂教学

两种叙事结构的比较，5 分钟

先打开课前的 preflight.md，确认编译和渲染已有结果，随后比较同一研究的两种写法。

初稿先介绍深度学习背景，读者要读到后文才知道研究发现。修订稿把网络先学会、后来退步的变化放在开头，随后依次回答三个问题：这一变化有多大，不同损失为何表现不同，替代方法能否保留早期学到的内容。

图的顺序与正文一致。第一张图呈现训练过程，第二张图比较不同噪声比例下的结果。曲线与标注都围绕主要比较安排，读者无需先辨认二十条线。

以核心发现组织论证，8 分钟

若只要求 Agent 罗列工作，输出往往是一份按操作顺序排列的清单。读者更关心研究改变了哪项认识。因此应先把核心发现写成一句可以复述的话，再决定各项证据的出场顺序。

范文在这里提供的是叙事结构：它怎样引出具体问题，给出回答，再把读者引向下一项证据。修订时用自己的材料重建这一结构，句子需要重新写，不能套用范文措辞。

数据核查与论文表达分开处理。重写可以调整段落位置，但数据的版本与来源必须保持不变。数字和图均由脚本从数据生成；编辑审阅再判断读者能否把握核心发现。

练习一：确定开篇并重写全文，17 分钟

在写作 Agent 中发送：

读 paper/main.tex 与 data/，先理解全文。
读课前存到 exemplar/ 的 arXiv 1611.03530 范文，分析题目、摘要和开篇怎样引出下一问。
列出本练习最值得开篇的三个发现，各带数据出处。选一个作为叙事中心，给三个题目。
用其中最清楚的题目，先写一份每段一句话的提纲，再另起全文到 versions/v1/main.tex。
图先沿用原图。编译 PDF，把提纲和编辑说明一并保存。原稿保留。

检验提纲的方法是只读各段首句，尝试复述论文。若复述的内容只是作者依次做了哪些工作，提纲仍需重排；若每段都在回答上一段留下的问题，即可展开全文。

课堂由 Agent 选定一个题目完成初稿，学生修改其中最关键的一处。本段结束时若尚未编译出 v1，可带着提纲和已完成章节进入下一段，用包内 preview/after.pdf 练习审阅；自己的完整 v1 在课后补审，并分别记录两份审阅对象。在正式论文中，题目与提纲可以单独提交讨论。

练习二：设计回答同一问题的数据图，13 分钟

读 code/plot_draft.py 和 style_table.md，用 data/ 重写 code/plot_paper.py。
每张图先写一句它要回答的问题。按论文实际栏宽输出矢量 PDF，保留全部种子的数据来源。
方法与颜色固定对应，说明均值和误差带的含义，把方法名放在读者能看清的位置。
输出到 versions/v1/figures/，更新本版论文引用。编译并逐页查看渲染结果，修复重叠与出界。

检查渲染页时以实际阅读尺寸为准，判断三项：最小字号能否看清，图注是否说明误差的含义，主结论是否来自图中画出的量。保留绘图脚本，数据更新后可直接重新生成。

练习三：独立审阅与方法留档，12 分钟

在另一家 Agent 的新会话中发送：

只读 versions/v1/main.pdf、style_table.md 与 exemplar/ 中的范文。
先读题目和摘要，判断你想继续追问什么；若抓不住中心，先指出卡住的具体位置。
再读全文，检查段落顺序和图文关系。每条意见定位到具体文本并给改进方向。
只写 versions/v1/review.md，注明审阅文件和判定，不改稿。
意见最后做主张检查：若要求缩小标题或核心主张，逐条说明必要证据；表达问题直接给重讲办法。

换用另一家 Agent 提供了不同视角；审阅的独立性还取决于输入范围，即审阅者只读这一版论文和范文，并独立作出判断。课堂先确定最需要处理的一条意见，课后由原作者据意见生成 versions/v2/，并保留逐条处理说明。

最后发送：

先把这次有效的改稿方法写成最小 paper-retell Skill。
内容涵盖范文分析与数据核查，以及整稿重讲和独立编辑审阅；调用已有脚本，去掉这次专用的文件名。
用新会话检查能否找到 Skill 和构建入口；完整迁移测试放在课后另一篇材料上。

课堂检查与本节小结，5 分钟

常见错误与处理方法

逐句润色后语言更通顺，论文仍缺少核心发现。 回到提纲，按读者会追问的顺序排列问题，再从头重写。

只要数字能在数据中找到，就判为正确。 同一数值可能来自另一组配置。核查记录应同时写明指标、配置与分母，详见 1.2。

只检查图文件，不检查成稿。 图放入栏中会被缩放。应在编译后查看整页，按最终尺寸判断可读性。

稿件已经换版，仍沿用旧审阅结论。 审阅意见绑定所审文件；新版本须重新提交审阅，审阅记录写明通过或退修。

本节小结

Python 从数据生成图，LaTeX 把源稿编译为 PDF，渲染器提供逐页检查所需的图片。AGENTS.md 记录工具入口，paper-retell 记录修订方法。下一次修订论文时，提供材料和范文后，Agent 即可按这些约定工作。

课后复习与扩展

概念复习、正反例分析及能力达成评价中的独立实践与迁移任务为本节必做内容；其余深入专题按研究需要选做。

概念复习，15 分钟

先不使用 Agent，用五句话复述自己的稿件：先陈述发现，再给出关键证据和比较，接着给出解释，最后提出下一个问题。然后打开课堂保存的版本，选一个数字追溯到数据行，说明它属于哪种设置。把复述内容与核心发现之间的差距写入 review-after-class.md。

正反例辨析，15 分钟

下面是两种练习提纲。甲先铺陈背景，接着罗列模型和实验，最后总结。乙从训练先变好后退步写起，再给出曲线、比较损失，最后解释差异。判断哪一份更能引出研究问题，并指出它还缺少哪项数据依据。另有两张图：一张只画最好的种子，另一张按同一设置保留全部种子。分别写一句读者能从图中得出的判断。

写完判断后，再查看参考思路

乙让每一段回答前一段提出的问题，但题目与曲线仍须对应同一实验。只画最好种子的图可以展示那一次运行的轨迹，不能据此估计典型表现；保留全部种子后，还要说明汇总方式与误差的口径。

扩展学习

1 Writing and figures

Learning outcomes

After completing this session and its review and extension work, students should be able to:

  1. Identify the central finding of a set of results and organize a paper’s argument so that each section answers the question raised by the one before.
  2. Verify that numbers, figures and text agree with the source data by specifying each reported value through its metric, configuration and aggregation.
  3. Design figures around an explicit research question, with uncertainty and scales suited to the comparison and legible at final printed size.
  4. Commission and use an independent review whose inputs, reviewed version and verdict are recorded.
  5. Abstract an effective revision method into a reusable Skill and test whether it transfers to different material.

The outcomes are assessed at three levels: the class exercises practice each method on a shared case, the review work requires students to complete the revision and checks independently, and the extension paths require the same methods to be applied to different material.

Preparation before class, a 60-minute class session, then required review, independent work and transfer assessment, with optional further study.

Preparation · Class session · Review and extension

A research paper is organized around what its results change in the reader’s understanding, not around the order in which the work was done. This session treats that principle as a method that can be checked: the central finding is stated first, the evidence is ordered by the questions a reader will ask, and every number and figure is traced to its data.

The case material is a four-page draft on learning with noisy labels. Its results show that when forty percent of the training labels are wrong, the network finds most of the correct answers early in training, and test performance declines as training continues. The draft places this finding behind general background and a list of methods, so the abstract does not tell the reader why the paper matters. The experiment has already been run locally, and the raw data and run scripts are included in the materials. The class works from the existing data and concentrates on presentation and verification.

Class session

Two presentations of the same research, 5 minutes

Confirm from preflight.md that compilation and rendering produced results, then compare the two versions.

The draft opens with background on deep learning, and the reader must read further to identify the central finding. The revised version opens with the observation that the network learns early and declines later, and then answers three questions in turn: how large the change is, why different losses behave differently, and whether alternatives can retain what was learned early.

The figures follow the same order. The first shows the training process and the second compares noise rates. Each line and label has a defined role, and the reader does not have to sort through twenty lines first.

Organizing a paper around one finding, 8 minutes

An agent asked to describe a piece of work tends to list what was done. Readers are more concerned with what the research changes in their understanding. The first step is therefore to state that finding in one sentence that a reader can remember, and then to decide which evidence must come first.

The exemplar is used to study narrative structure: how it establishes a specific research question, how it gives the answer, and how it leads the reader to the next piece of evidence. That order is then rebuilt with the case material, and the sentences are written anew rather than adapted.

Numerical verification is kept separate from the narrative. Restructuring may move paragraphs, but the underlying data and their provenance must not change. Numbers come from scripts and figures from data; the editorial review then judges whether a reader can grasp the finding.

Exercise 1: selecting the opening and restructuring the draft, 17 minutes

In the writing agent, send:

Read paper/main.tex and data/, and understand the whole paper first.
Read the arXiv 1611.03530 exemplar saved to exemplar/ before class, and analyze how its title, abstract and opening lead into the next question.
List the three findings in this exercise most worth opening with, each with its data source. Choose one as the narrative center and give three titles.
Using the clearest title, first write an outline with one sentence per paragraph, then rewrite the complete paper in versions/v1/main.tex.
Keep the original figures for now. Compile the PDF and save the outline and editing notes with it. Keep the original draft.

To evaluate the outline, summarize the paper from its paragraph openings alone. If the summary reproduces the sequence of things the authors did, the outline needs reordering; if each paragraph answers the question left by the one before, the full text can be written.

In class, the agent selects the title and completes the first draft, and the student revises the most important point. If v1 has not compiled by the end of this block, carry the outline and finished sections into the next block and practice the review on the packaged preview/after.pdf; the student’s complete v1 is then reviewed after class, and both review targets are recorded. In a real paper, the title and outline may be discussed separately before drafting.

Exercise 2: designing figures that answer the same question, 13 minutes

Read code/plot_draft.py and style_table.md, and rewrite code/plot_paper.py using data/.
For each figure, first write one sentence stating the question it answers. Output vector PDFs at the paper's actual column width, keeping the data source for all seeds.
Give each method a fixed color, state what the mean and the error band mean, and put method names where readers can see them.
Output to versions/v1/figures/ and update this version's figure references. Compile and look at the rendered result page by page, fixing overlaps and anything out of bounds.

Inspect the pages at actual reading size. The criteria are whether the smallest text is legible, whether the caption explains the error measure, and whether the main conclusion follows from the quantity plotted. The plotting script is retained so that the figures can be regenerated when the data change.

Exercise 3: independent review and recording the method, 12 minutes

In a new session of the other vendor’s agent, send:

Read only versions/v1/main.pdf, style_table.md and the exemplar in exemplar/.
Read the title and abstract first and decide what you want to ask next; if the main finding is unclear, first point to the exact place where you got stuck.
Then read the full paper and check paragraph order and how figures relate to the text. Tie every comment to specific text and give a direction for improvement.
Write only versions/v1/review.md, naming the file reviewed and your verdict. Do not change the draft.
End your comments with a claim check: if you ask to narrow the title or a core claim, give the evidence that makes it necessary for each one; for problems of expression, suggest how to restructure the text.

An agent from another vendor brings a different perspective, but independence also depends on the scope of the input: the reviewer sees only this version and the exemplar and forms its own judgment. In class, identify the comment that most needs to be addressed. After class, the original author produces versions/v2/ from the comments and records how each comment was handled.

Finally, send:

First write the revision method that worked this time into a minimal paper-retell Skill.
Cover exemplar analysis, number checking, restructuring the complete draft and independent editorial review; call the existing scripts and drop the file names specific to this run.
Use a new session to check that the Skill and the build command can be found; the full transfer test is done after class on another set of materials.

Common errors and summary, 5 minutes

Common errors and corrections

Polishing every sentence while the paper still lacks a center. Return to the outline, order the reader’s follow-up questions and rewrite the full paper.

Accepting a number because it appears somewhere in the data. The same value may belong to another group. The verification record must state the metric, the configuration and the denominator together; see 1.2.

Inspecting the figure file rather than the finished page. A figure is scaled when placed in a column. Inspect the whole page after compilation and judge legibility at final size.

Applying a review to a draft that has since changed. Comments refer to the file reviewed. A new version is reviewed again, and the review record states whether it passed or was returned for revision.

Method summary

Python connects data to figures, LaTeX connects source to PDF, and the renderer produces page images for visual inspection. AGENTS.md records the entry points and the paper-retell Skill records the method. In a later revision, the agent is given the materials and an exemplar and can apply the recorded method.

Review and extension

Conceptual review, analysis of contrasting examples, and the independent and transfer tasks specified in Assessment are required for this session. Other extension topics are optional, except for the required readings identified below.

Unaided review, 15 minutes

Without using the agent, summarize the draft in five sentences: the finding first, then the key evidence and comparison, then the explanation, and finally the next question. Then open the version saved in class, trace one number back to its data row and state which setting it belongs to. Record omissions or errors in the summary in review-after-class.md.

Analysis of contrasting examples, 15 minutes

Two outlines of the exercise paper are given. Outline A presents background, then lists models and experiments, and ends with a summary. Outline B starts from training that first improves and then deteriorates, then presents the curves, compares losses and finally explains the difference. Decide which better exposes the research question, and identify any supporting data that are missing. Two figures are also given: one plots only the best seed, the other keeps all seeds under the same setting. For each, write one sentence on what a reader can conclude from it.

Record your judgment before reading the commentary

B lets each paragraph answer the question raised by the one before, but its title and curves must still refer to the same experiment. A figure of the best seed alone shows that run’s trajectory but cannot be used to estimate typical performance; once all seeds are kept, the figure must still state how results are aggregated and what the error measure means.

Further study

The class should have produced a versioned draft and a review record. After class, first produce the next version from the review comments, then choose one path. When the exercise is repeated with students’ own materials, the aim remains to restructure the complete draft and verify its numbers, rather than to substitute new nouns into the example.

Path Next task Deliverable
1.1 Structuring a paper and 1.4 Peer review and revision 45 plus 30 minutes: turn review comments into a new narrative New full version and point-by-point responses
1.2 Checking the numbers and 1.3 Plotting results 35 plus 45 minutes: change one data row and rebuild numbers and figures Data identity, scripts and a check of the actual compiled paper
1.5 Reusable methods 25 minutes: test paper-retell on another set of materials The Skill and one transfer test
1.6 Citations and submission 30 minutes: prepare a submission package for one target venue Citation check and delivery checklist

Assessment

Attainment of the outcomes is judged from the student’s explanation of the method, not from the existence of the files. Students should be able to state the central finding of their revised paper and justify why the chosen evidence comes first; trace any reported number to its metric, configuration and data rows; explain what each figure allows a reader to conclude; and state which review verdict applies to which version. Transfer is assessed by applying the same methods to a different paper or to the paper-retell Skill on other material, and reporting where the method held and where it had to be changed.
