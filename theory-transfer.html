6.3 原点漂移与迁移

课后深入，约 60 分钟。

一张榜在上周有用，这周收益消失。可能是原点升级，也可能是任务换了，或评审尺度变了。三种变化同时发生，单看一条总分曲线就分不清。

原点的完整记录

闭源 Agent 包括模型与厂商执行循环，还依赖可见工具和权限。记录产品版本与模型标识，注明可选参数、工具清单与执行时间，并保存能够取得的服务端信息。

厂商未暴露的版本无法强行固定。用一组不参与学习的锚定任务定期测无经验表现，观测到变化后分段统计。锚定集用于漂移诊断，不能反复拿来挑经验。

建一张迁移矩阵

在原点 A 学出 BAB_A，在原点 B 学出 BBB_B，两份榜冻结。分别给两个原点评估：

经验来源 原点 A 执行 原点 B 执行
A 学出的榜 JA(BA)J_A(B_A) JB(BA)J_B(B_A)
B 学出的榜 JA(BB)J_A(B_B) JB(BB)J_B(B_B)

每个执行原点都另有无经验和匹配安慰剂。迁移看相对本原点基线的增量，避免把原点 B 本来更强解释成榜迁移成功。

源榜收益接近零时，迁移率比值会很不稳定。优先报各格差值和区间，比例作为有适用条件的辅助量。

漂移对收益的影响可以界定

固定任务分布、预算与评审，对考虑的经验集合定义：

D=supB|Jv′(B)−Jv(B)|.D=\sup_B|J_{v'}(B)-J_v(B)|.

令某张榜的无经验增量为 Δv(B)=Jv(B)−Jv(⌀)\Delta_v(B)=J_v(B)-J_v(\varnothing)，由三角不等式：

|Δv′(B)−Δv(B)|≤2D.|\Delta_{v'}(B)-\Delta_v(B)|\le 2D.

这条界说，两次原点表现的变化会同时进入有榜与无榜两项。实际只测有限的榜，得到的是这些榜上的漂移读数，不能把样本最大差直接当成整个经验空间的已知上界。

经验迁移的可检验预测

可以提出三条相互竞争的预测：通用操作经验被更强原点吸收，领域新规律仍有增量，绑定某种工具行为的经验随接口变化失效。

先按经验来源与动作类型分层，再看新旧原点上的条目消融。原点更强并不意味着每条经验的收益必然降低；新能力也可能让原来无法执行的经验变得可用。

因此，可以检验这样一条预测：原点越强，通用经验的收益越低。用同一张榜、共同任务与一致评审测新旧版本，再增加训练截止之后的新知识条目作比较。

固定尺度的跨范式比较

要比较闭源原点外的经验学习与开放模型的参数学习，可以做一个二维设计。模型来源一轴，学习方式另一轴。开放模型也加经验榜，才能把原点能力和更新方式的作用分开。

公平性至少报告两套预算：相同调用或采样次数，以及相同总资源成本。参数训练的预算包含训练开销；经验学习的预算则包括写榜与检索、搜索与评估。两套结果一并报告，研究主张继续由这个完整比较支撑。

练习

用包内演算数据，比较同一张榜在两种原点记录下的内容差与总效应。用两次再评估的差异，定位需要重新测试的条目。

再设计自己的迁移矩阵，写明哪一格用来测试保值，哪一格用来分开原点能力，哪项观测会要求重新学榜。

常见错误与处理方法

新原点总分高，就宣布旧榜有用。 比较该原点自己的无经验与安慰剂增量。

榜、任务和评审同时更新。 冻结其中两项，桥接比较第三项，变化分别留身份。

迁移比值分母很小仍强调倍数。 先报绝对差和不确定性，再解释比例。

对照 SkillOpt 的技能优化，再回到原点边界、迁移设置和成本口径逐项比较。

6.3 Origin drift and transfer

After-class deep dive, about 60 minutes.

A playbook helped last week, and this week the gain is gone. The origin may have been upgraded, the tasks may have changed, or the scoring criteria may have changed. When all three change at once, one curve of total scores cannot tell them apart.

Record the origin as a whole

A closed-source agent includes the model and the vendor’s execution loop, and also depends on the visible tools and permissions. Record the product version, model identifier, optional parameters, tool list and time of execution, and save whatever server-side information you can get.

You cannot pin a version that the vendor does not expose. Use a set of anchor tasks that take no part in learning to measure no-playbook performance regularly, and once a change is observed, compute statistics by segment. The anchor set is for drift diagnosis and must not be used again and again to choose experience.

Build a transfer matrix

Learn BAB_A on origin A and BBB_B on origin B, and freeze both playbooks. Evaluate each on both origins:

Source of experience Run on origin A Run on origin B
Playbook learned on A JA(BA)J_A(B_A) JB(BA)J_B(B_A)
Playbook learned on B JA(BB)J_A(B_B) JB(BB)J_B(B_B)

Each executing origin also has its own no-playbook and matched-placebo groups. Transfer looks at the increment over that origin’s own baseline, so that origin B simply being stronger is not read as the playbook transferring successfully.

When the source playbook’s gain is close to zero, a transfer ratio becomes very unstable. Report the difference and interval in each cell first, and treat the ratio as an auxiliary quantity with conditions of use.

The effect of drift on gains can be bounded

Fix the task distribution, budget and reviewer, and for the set of experience under consideration define:

D=supB|Jv′(B)−Jv(B)|.D=\sup_B|J_{v'}(B)-J_v(B)|.

Let a playbook’s increment over no playbook be Δv(B)=Jv(B)−Jv(⌀)\Delta_v(B)=J_v(B)-J_v(\varnothing). By the triangle inequality:

|Δv′(B)−Δv(B)|≤2D.|\Delta_{v'}(B)-\Delta_v(B)|\le 2D.

This bound says that a change in origin performance enters both the with-playbook and the no-playbook terms. In practice you measure only a finite number of playbooks, which gives an estimate of drift on those playbooks; the largest difference in the sample cannot be taken as a known upper bound for the whole space of experience.

Test which experience retains its value

You can put forward three competing predictions: general operating experience is absorbed by stronger origins, new regularities in a domain still add something, and experience tied to some tool behavior stops working when the interface changes.

First stratify by source of experience and type of action, then look at entry ablations on the old and new origins. A stronger origin does not mathematically force every entry to lose value; new capabilities may also make previously unusable experience actionable.

So take “the stronger the origin, the less general experience is worth” as a testable prediction. Test the old and new versions with the same playbook, shared tasks and a consistent reviewer, then add entries of new knowledge from after the training cutoff for comparison.

Cross-paradigm comparison at a fixed scale

To compare experience learning outside a closed-source origin with parameter learning in an open model, you can use a two-dimensional design. Model source is one axis and learning method the other. Only when the open model also gets a playbook can you separate the effect of origin capability from that of the update method.

For fairness, report at least two budgets: the same number of calls or samples, and the same total resource cost. Parameter training includes the training overhead; experience learning includes writing the playbook, retrieval, search and evaluation. Report both sets of results together, and use this full comparison to support the research claim.

Exercise

Using the worked-example data in the package, compare the content difference and total effect of the same playbook under the two origin records. Use the difference between the two re-evaluations to locate the entries that need retesting.

Then design your own transfer matrix, stating which cell tests whether value is kept, which cell separates origin capability, and which observation would require learning the playbook again.

Common mistakes and how to fix them

Declaring the old playbook useful because the new origin’s total score is higher. Compare the increments over that origin’s own no-playbook and placebo groups.

Playbook, tasks and reviewer all updated at once. Freeze two of them, use bridging tasks to compare the third, and give each change its own identity.

Emphasizing a ratio when the transfer ratio’s denominator is tiny. Report the absolute difference and its uncertainty first, then interpret the ratio.
