Agent 科研

一篇稿子改得顺了，数字却还停在旧版本。一批论文读完了，问起某条结论的出处，又要从头翻。实验顺利跑了一夜，早上才发现漏掉了能区分两种解释的对照。

Agent 能加快这些工作。研究者要学会让每一步留下依据，供后续工作使用。这门课从一篇四页论文开始，带你搭起一个自己的科研工作台。你会练习用 Agent 重讲论文并核查数字，阅读文献时追问证据，在实验前明确结果的判断标准。

四堂必修按顺序上，每堂课分三段：课前备好材料，课上一小时共同解决难题，课后从复习走向深入。第 5、6 课为选修，分别讲安全控制与编排理论，也沿用同样的节奏。

0 课前准备 怎样让 Agent 在正确的文件夹里开始工作 两个能用的 Agent，一份会议纪要和核查记录

AI Agents for Research

Build each research step on evidence

A draft finally reads well, but its numbers are still from an old version. You have read a batch of papers, and when someone asks where a conclusion came from, you start searching again. An experiment ran smoothly overnight, and in the morning you notice it lacked the control that could separate two explanations.

Agents can speed up these tasks. Researchers need to ensure that each step produces evidence the next step can use. Starting with a four-page paper, this course helps you build your own research workbench. You will use an agent to restructure a paper and check its numbers, read the literature and examine its evidence, and decide before each experiment how its results will be judged.

Take the four required lessons in order. Each has three parts: preparation before class, a one-hour class for working through the harder problems together, and review followed by optional further study. Lessons 5 and 6 are electives on safety controls and orchestration theory, with the same structure.

Lesson The question it answers What you take away
0 Getting started How to get an agent working in the right folder Two working agents, meeting minutes and a fact-check report
1 Writing and figures How to present the same research clearly and engagingly A restructured draft, two data figures and a revision Skill
2 Research knowledge bases How papers you have read can support a new question A knowledge base linked to source papers, and questions to test
3 Experimental analysis Which experiment can separate two explanations Predictions recorded in advance, paired results and a reproducible record
4 Research automation How one round of research can lead into the next by itself A recoverable research loop and candidate playbook entries to test
5 Safety controls How to keep control of permissions when the agent reads outside content A permission boundary you can test, and automatic checks
6 Orchestration theory How a system learns while the agent’s weights stay fixed A formal account of learning from experience and a controlled experiment plan

How every lesson runs

Stage What you do What is left at the end
Before class Install software, download dependencies and materials, log in, then run a minimal working check preflight.md and accessible local materials
In class Analyze the problem together, configure complex rules, run short exercises, then check the results A versioned output with recorded sources
After class: review and extension Recall and check first, then compare examples, then choose one path to go deeper A review record and one connected extension exercise

Tools accumulate from lesson to lesson. Lesson 1 sets up compilation and plotting, Lesson 2 adds a browser and a knowledge base, Lesson 3 brings in version control and background execution, and Lesson 4 connects scheduling and results pages. Complete installations and downloads in advance. In class, we work through how to use the tools in research and which judgments are essential. Record tool commands in the project files so a new session can continue the work. The Toolbox lists the tools for each stage.

The course is for graduate students and their supervisors. Lesson 1 starts with files and completed examples; later lessons develop your ability to judge evidence, design controls, and derive the conditions under which a learning system works. When you are ready, start with Lesson 0. The teaching plan is in the Syllabus.
