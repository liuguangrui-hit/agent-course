2 知识库

把文献中的分歧转化为可用实验检验的问题。课堂完成三篇论文的证据链，写出一个带竞争解释的研究问题；课后扩到材料清单中的十四篇，再留下三个候选问题。

课前完成准备，课上 60 分钟，课后先复习再选做深入。

课前预备 · 课上一小时 · 课后复习与拔高

两篇论文都在带噪标签下比较鲁棒损失，方法的排名却反过来了。Agent 很容易把它们压成一句各有优劣。研究从追问开始：数据相同吗，学习率日程相同吗，取的是最后一轮还是最好一轮？

你需要一个能把这些问题追回原文的知识库。关系图帮助查找材料，证据表则说明材料能支持哪些判断。

下载第 2 课材料

papers.md 给出十四篇核对过身份的论文。preview/ 是六篇实读笔记组成的示例库，未包含受限全文。课堂选三篇，全文在课前取得；缺失项提前报告。预览可用于证据组织演练，原件核查另记待补。

课上一小时

查看分歧记录，5 分钟

先看 preflight.md，说明用自己的原件还是用预览演练，再进入同一条分歧。

示例库的 conflicts.md 记录 SCE 与 GCE 的排序反转，也保留了两篇论文各自的设置和页码。它没有把一切差异归到某一个原因：学习率和其他训练参数也变了。

这张表提供一个实验入口。在同一实现里固定其他设置，只改日程，看看排序是否随之变化。相比摘要里一句性能优越，这个问题能直接决定下一项工作。

论文页、概念页与问题页，10 分钟

论文页记录原作者在具体设置下观察到什么。概念页综合多篇论文，说明哪些认识已经相互支持，哪些依赖条件。问题页再提出你的解释，并写出能区分它与另一种解释的实验。

三层用链接连接。原文观察、跨论文归纳和自己的推测各有身份，更新时就知道该动哪一层。一个概念页写得流畅，还需要逐条能追到论文页。

练习一，核对三份原件，10 分钟

从 papers.md 选编号 2，5，11，核对对应 PDF 的标题，作者与版本。
保存到 vault/sources/，记录来源 URL，取得时间，SHA-256 和 PDF 页数。
标明后面页码按 PDF 顺序页还是印刷页计。
若缺全文，记录可用入口与当前原因，先用 preview/ 中对应笔记练习，不冒充已读全文。

同一篇论文的不同版本可以共存，记录各自身份。版本不同，表号和数字也可能不同。

练习二，分工阅读并建立证据表，20 分钟

三篇论文分给独立阅读助手，每个只读指定文件，写一份论文页。
每项结果记录设置，指标，数值与原文位置。原作者解释单独写，自己的推测另列。
主会话抽查一条核心结果与一条方法条件，再合并为一个鲁棒损失概念页。
把不能直接比较的条件列入 conflicts.md，说明需要怎样的同条件实验。

助手只交文件位置和未解决项。主会话遇到关键矛盾，回读相关原文。最后由一个会话负责合并概念页，避免多个写作者覆盖同一结论。

练习三，从分歧走到可检验问题，10 分钟

只读当前知识库，提出一个研究问题。
写两种竞争解释，分别预测哪项可测结果会不同。
给出最小实验、主要指标和事前判据，注明来自哪些证据行。
另请一个新会话先找最近的一项先例，分别比较问题与干预，再核对指标和结论，其余查新留到 2.3。
存为 questions.md 和 novelty.md。

课堂结束时看一件事：若实验结果呈现 A 的预测，你会更新哪条认识；若呈现 B 的预测，又会更新哪条。两种结果都能更新知识库，问题就有继续做的价值。

检查与工作台小结，5 分钟

常见错误与处理方法

把设置不同的结果叫矛盾。 先把比较单位对齐。暂时无法对齐的，记录为条件差异，再设计同条件实验。

把笔记写成结论的二次来源。 概念页保留通向原文的链，关键判断回读原文抽查。

让引用数决定全部阅读名单。 先按问题覆盖方法与负结果，再看引用关系补漏，新工作另开一轮检索。

查新后只说没有完全一样的工作。 给出最近先例的逐项对照，说明新实验会多回答哪一问。

工作台小结

浏览器用于获取外部原件，文献接口提供可复查标识，Obsidian 让你与 Agent 共读同一批文件，Sub-agent 让独立阅读可以扩展。现在可追溯的单位是一条带设置的证据，下一课据此设计实验。

课后复习与拔高

先复盘，15 分钟

关掉概念页，画出课堂的一条证据链：从原件到论文页，再到概念页与问题页。凭记忆写每一步负责什么，再打开文件核对。抽一条结果回到原文，检查版本、页码和设置，补完课堂标为待核的项目，写入 review-after-class.md。

正反例辨析，20 分钟

甲笔记写两篇论文排名相反，所以机制互相否定。乙笔记把日程、噪声和选择轮次分别列出，提出固定其他设置只改日程的实验。判断两份笔记各能支持哪一层结论。再给乙增加一篇反例，沿链接列出哪些页面需要重新判断，哪些原文记录应当保留。

写完更新清单，再看对照思路

甲把条件差异直接当成机制冲突。乙先记录观察，再提出可区分的解释。反例进入后，原件和历史观察保留，当前概念、竞争解释与实验问题按依赖关系复查；改了一个概念标题不算完成更新。

沿课堂往深处做

先把课堂三篇补成完整证据链，再选一条路径。扩到十四篇时沿用同一模板，在自己的方向上最后留下三个各有竞争解释和判别指标的问题。

2 Research knowledge bases

Turn a disagreement in the literature into a question you can test with an experiment. In class you build the evidence chain for three papers and write one research question with competing explanations; after class you extend to the fourteen papers in the materials list and develop three candidate questions.

Prepare before class, spend 60 minutes in class, then review and choose an optional extension.

Before class · In class · After class: review and extension

Two papers both compare robust losses under noisy labels, yet their rankings of the methods are reversed. An agent may reduce this to a vague summary: each has strengths and weaknesses. Research starts by asking further: were the data the same, was the learning-rate schedule the same, did they take the last epoch or the best one?

You need a knowledge base that traces these questions back to the originals. The graph helps you find related material; the evidence table shows which conclusions it can support.

Download the Lesson 2 materials

papers.md lists fourteen papers whose identities have been checked. preview/ is a sample knowledge base containing notes from six papers read in full, and it contains no restricted full texts. The class chooses three papers whose full texts you get before class; report anything missing in advance. The preview can be used to rehearse organizing evidence, with checks against the originals recorded as still to be done.

In class

Documenting a disagreement, 5 minutes

First look at preflight.md and say whether you are using your own originals or rehearsing with the preview, then move to the same disagreement.

The sample knowledge base’s conflicts.md records the reversed ranking of SCE and GCE and keeps each paper’s settings and page numbers. It does not attribute every difference to a single cause: the learning rate and other training parameters also changed.

This table offers an entry point for an experiment. In one implementation, fix the other settings, change only the schedule, and see whether the ranking changes with it. Compared with “superior performance” in an abstract, this question directly decides the next piece of work.

Separate evidence into three layers, 10 minutes

A paper page records what the original authors observed under specific settings. A concept page synthesizes several papers, identifying conclusions supported across studies and those that depend on particular conditions. A question page then proposes your explanation and describes an experiment that can distinguish it from another explanation.

The three layers are connected by links. Original observations, cross-paper generalizations and your own speculation are recorded separately, so when something is updated you know which layer to touch. A concept page that reads smoothly still has to trace back, line by line, to paper pages.

Exercise 1: check the three originals, 10 minutes

From papers.md, choose items 2, 5 and 11 and check the title, authors and version of each corresponding PDF.
Save them to vault/sources/, recording the source URL, time obtained, SHA-256 and PDF page count.
State whether later page numbers follow the PDF page order or the printed page numbers.
If a full text is missing, record the available entry point and the current reason, and practice first with the corresponding note in preview/, without pretending the full text has been read.

Different versions of the same paper can coexist, each with its own recorded identity. When versions differ, table numbers and values may differ too.

Exercise 2: split the reading and build the evidence table, 20 minutes

Give the three papers to independent reading helpers. Each reads only its assigned file and writes one paper page.
For each result, record the setting, metric, value and location in the original. Write the original authors' explanation separately, and list my own speculation separately again.
The main session spot-checks one core result and one method condition, then merges them into a single concept page on robust losses.
List conditions that cannot be compared directly in conflicts.md, stating what experiment under matched conditions is needed.

Helpers hand in only file locations and unresolved items. When the main session meets a key contradiction, it rereads the relevant original. In the end one session is responsible for merging the concept page, so several writers do not overwrite the same conclusion.

Exercise 3: from disagreement to a testable question, 10 minutes

Read only the current knowledge base and propose one research question.
Write two competing explanations, and for each predict which measurable result would differ.
Give the minimal experiment, the main metric and the criteria set in advance, noting which evidence rows they come from.
Also ask a new session to find the single nearest precedent and compare it item by item on question, intervention, metric and conclusion; leave the rest of the novelty search for 2.3.
Save as questions.md and novelty.md.

At the end of class, look at one thing: if the experiment shows A’s prediction, which conclusion would you update; if it shows B’s prediction, what would change? If either result would change the knowledge base, the question is worth pursuing.

Check and workbench summary, 5 minutes

Common mistakes and how to fix them

Calling results from different settings a contradiction. Align the unit of comparison first. What cannot be aligned yet is recorded as a difference in conditions, then design an experiment under matched conditions.

Writing notes as a secondary source of conclusions. Concept pages keep the chain to the originals, and key judgments are spot-checked by rereading the original.

Letting citation counts decide the whole reading list. First cover methods and negative results by question, then fill gaps through citation relations, and run a separate search for new work.

Saying only “no identical work exists” after a novelty search. Give an item-by-item comparison with the nearest precedent and say which extra question the new experiment answers.

Workbench summary

The browser connects you to external originals, literature APIs leave identifiers that can be rechecked, Obsidian lets you and the agent read the same files, and Sub-agents let independent reading scale. The traceable unit is now a piece of evidence with its setting, and the next lesson turns it into an experiment.

After class: review and extension

Review first, 15 minutes

Close the concept page and draw one evidence chain from class: from the original to the paper page, then to the concept page and the question page. From memory, write what each step is responsible for, then open the files and check. Take one result back to the original, check the version, page and setting, finish the items marked as pending in class, and write it all into review-after-class.md.

Compare the examples, 20 minutes

Note A says the two papers rank the methods in opposite order, so the papers’ proposed mechanisms contradict each other. Note B lists the schedule, the noise and the selected epoch separately, and proposes an experiment that fixes the other settings and changes only the schedule. Judge which level of conclusion each note can support. Then add a counterexample paper to B, and following the links, list which pages need to be judged again and which records of the originals should be kept.

Write your update list first, then see the comparison notes

A treats a difference in conditions directly as a conflict between mechanisms. B records observations first, then proposes explanations that can be told apart. When the counterexample comes in, the originals and past observations are kept, and the current synthesis, competing explanations and experiment questions are rechecked along their dependencies; changing one concept title does not count as finishing the update.

Further study

First complete the evidence chain for the three class papers, then choose one path. When extending to fourteen papers, use the same template, and in your own field end with three questions, each with competing explanations and a discriminating metric.
