6.1 经验学习与策略表示

学习目标

  1. 在固定原点下定义经验集合诱导的策略类、单步与序贯目标，以及回报驱动的更新规则。
  2. 运用有限候选集的集中不等式估计评估所需的任务量，并说明自适应选择使该保证失效的条件。
  3. 设计条目消融与回报打乱对照，分析条目间交互作用和更新过程的收敛条件。

课后深入专题，约 75 分钟。

一条经验能够改变行为，就成为策略的一部分。至于经验究竟学到了什么，需要分别从策略类、目标与更新规则加以说明。

固定原点与可行经验

令 ℬK,L\mathcal B_{K,L} 为至多 KK 条、总长不超过 LL 的经验集合。原点 𝒜v\mathcal A_v 固定为一个可观测的版本，由此得到策略类：

Πv={πB:B∈ℬK,L},πB(y∣x)=𝒜v(y∣x,B).\Pi_v=\{\pi_B:B\in\mathcal B_{K,L}\},\qquad \pi_B(y\mid x)=\mathcal A_v(y\mid x,B).

单步任务分布为 DD，回报有界且方向固定，例如 r∈[0,1]r\in[0,1]，越大越好。目标为：

Jv(B)=𝔼x∼D,y∼πB[r(x,y)],B*∈arg⁡maxBJv(B).J_v(B)=\mathbb E_{x\sim D,\,y\sim\pi_B}[r(x,y)],\qquad B^*\in\arg\max_B J_v(B).

原点在无经验条件下的表现为 Jv(⌀)J_v(\varnothing)。经验带来的增量须在同一任务分布与执行预算下比较。经验榜的容量约束给出明确的搜索空间，也使不同方法的输入成本可以比较。

更新规则中的评价与选择

Bt+1=Uϕ(Bt,xt,yt,rt).B_{t+1}=U_\phi(B_t,x_t,y_t,r_t).

UϕU_\phi 可以先提议新增条目，再用验证任务比较候选；也可以先从失败中提炼多种做法，分别试验后选择。是否使用梯度，并不决定它是否构成回报驱动的策略学习。关键在于反馈怎样影响更新，以及目标是否指向未来回报。

为检验这一点，可训练一个使用打乱回报的更新器作为对照。回报须在训练任务之间打乱，不能只在最终计分时打乱。两者在同一正常评估集上的差异，才反映正确反馈的作用。

单步与序贯问题

在论文回放中，每份材料对应一次预测，作答不影响下一篇材料，适合先建立情境老虎机模型。研究执行则有状态 sts_t、动作 ata_t 与转移 PP：

Jv(B)=𝔼πB,P[∑t=0T−1γtrt].J_v(B)=\mathbb E_{\pi_B,P}\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^t r_t\right].

动作可能是选择一个实验，实验结果改变已知证据，再决定下一个实验。延迟到来的回报需要分配给之前的选择。可以把预算内的最终判别正确率作为主要目标，把运行成功率与成本作为单独的指标，以免系统只学会挑选容易完成的任务。

若只从最终成功的轨迹中提取经验，会遗漏因选择不当而过早失败的轨迹。训练记录应包含失败与终止原因。

有限候选集的保证

先考虑事先固定的 MM 张候选榜。每张榜在 nn 个独立任务上测量有界回报，这些任务未参与生成这组候选。由 Hoeffding 不等式与并合界，以至少 1−δ1-\delta 的概率，所有候选同时满足：

|Ĵ(B)−J(B)|≤ϵ=log⁡(2M/δ)2n.|\widehat J(B)-J(B)|\le \epsilon=\sqrt{\frac{\log(2M/\delta)}{2n}}.

因此，按平均分选出的榜与这组候选中最优榜的真实回报之差至多为 2ϵ2\epsilon。反之，要把每张榜的误差控制在 ϵ\epsilon 以内，需要：

n≥log⁡(2M/δ)2ϵ2.n\ge\frac{\log(2M/\delta)}{2\epsilon^2}.

这是固定候选、独立评估条件下的比较保证。若不断读取验证成绩再生成新榜，候选就依赖于这些任务，需要新的确认集，或采用能覆盖自适应选择的分析。

文本越长，可表达的候选越多。容量约束把表达能力与估计难度联系起来，但上述界通常相当保守，实际仍需画出样本量与稳定性的关系。

有限状态不蕴含单调改进

把经验榜与更新器状态合在一起，在固定任务分布与更新规则下，可以分析由此形成的随机过程。即使只有两张榜，若每次更新都切换到另一张，过程也会来回振荡。

要证明收敛，还须说明转移结构；要证明收敛到高回报状态，还须说明选择机制与目标之间的关系。候选验证、保留当前最好版本和独立确认各有作用，每一项都须对应明确的假设。

条目信用分配与消融

对条目 eie_i，可以比较完整榜与移除该条目后的榜：

Δi=J(B)−J(B\{ei}).\Delta_i=J(B)-J(B\setminus\{e_i\}).

用等长的安慰剂替换被移除的部分，可以减少篇幅变化带来的混淆。多个条目之间存在交互作用，Δi\Delta_i 依赖于当前的榜，不能把各条的分数简单相加作为全榜收益。

声明使用了哪条经验，只是可观察的线索。随机关闭条目、替换条目内容或成组消融，才是对原因的干预。

练习

设有八张事先确定的候选榜，回报介于零到一之间。取 δ=0.05\delta=0.05，计算使每张榜误差不超过 0.1 所需的任务量，再与每张只测六题的情形比较。解释为何小规模试跑适合发现设计问题，却不足以判定小幅收益。

再设计一个两条经验相互依赖的例子，说明单条消融的结果为何不能直接相加。包内的 theory_checks.py 会复算样本量与两状态振荡。

常见错误与处理方法

写出上确界即宣称算法达到上界。 分别报告可行空间、搜索方法和实际达到的经验回报。

把同一题的多次采样当作多个独立任务。 按任务聚类，区分任务间泛化与任务内随机性。

发现一条经验有用，即认为全榜每条都有贡献。 对候选条目做消融，并保留交互作用。

理论背景可从 Sutton 与 Barto 的 Reinforcement Learning中关于策略与回报的定义读起，再对照 Reflexion 和 GEPA 的实际更新方式。

6.1 Learning through experience

Learning outcomes

After this section, students should be able to:

  1. Define the policy class, objective and update rule of a learning system built on a fixed origin, for both single-step and sequential tasks.
  2. Derive and apply the finite-candidate comparison bound to plan evaluation sample sizes, and state the conditions under which it holds.
  3. Design interventions, such as shuffled-reward training and entry ablations, that test the role of feedback and the contribution of individual entries.

Extension reading and exercises, about 75 minutes.

Once a playbook entry can change behavior, it has become part of the policy. An account of what is learned therefore requires a definition of the policy class, the objective and the update rule.

Fixing the origin and defining the admissible playbooks

Let ℬK,L\mathcal B_{K,L} be the set of playbooks with at most KK entries and total length at most LL. Fixing the origin 𝒜v\mathcal A_v to an observable version gives the policy class:

Πv={πB:B∈ℬK,L},πB(y∣x)=𝒜v(y∣x,B).\Pi_v=\{\pi_B:B\in\mathcal B_{K,L}\},\qquad \pi_B(y\mid x)=\mathcal A_v(y\mid x,B).

The single-step task distribution is DD, and the reward is bounded with a fixed direction, for example r∈[0,1]r\in[0,1], higher is better. The objective is:

Jv(B)=𝔼x∼D,y∼πB[r(x,y)],B*∈arg⁡maxBJv(B).J_v(B)=\mathbb E_{x\sim D,\,y\sim\pi_B}[r(x,y)],\qquad B^*\in\arg\max_B J_v(B).

The origin’s performance without experience is Jv(⌀)J_v(\varnothing). The increment due to experience is compared under the same task distribution and execution budget. The capacity constraint on the playbook defines an explicit search space and also gives different methods comparable input costs.

The update rule also requires evaluation and selection

Bt+1=Uϕ(Bt,xt,yt,rt).B_{t+1}=U_\phi(B_t,x_t,y_t,r_t).

UϕU_\phi can propose new entries and then compare candidates on validation tasks; it can also first distill several methods from failures, try each and then choose. Whether it uses gradients does not determine whether it constitutes reward-driven policy learning. What matters is how feedback affects the update and whether the objective concerns future reward.

To test this, an updater trained with shuffled rewards serves as a control. Rewards are shuffled across training tasks, not only when scores are computed at the end. The two are compared on the same unshuffled evaluation set to assess the role of correct feedback.

Single-step and sequential problems

In paper replay, each piece of material receives one prediction and the answer does not change the next paper, so a contextual bandit is a useful first model. Research execution has a state sts_t, an action ata_t and a transition PP:

Jv(B)=𝔼πB,P[∑t=0T−1γtrt].J_v(B)=\mathbb E_{\pi_B,P}\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^t r_t\right].

An action may be the choice of an experiment; the experimental result changes the available evidence, which in turn determines the next experiment. A delayed reward must be assigned to earlier choices. The rate of correct final discrimination within budget can serve as the main objective, with run success and cost treated as separate metrics, so that the system does not simply learn to select tasks that are easy to complete.

Distilling experience only from trajectories that eventually succeeded misses trajectories that failed early because of poor choices. Training records should include failures and the reasons for termination.

What a finite candidate set can guarantee

Consider first MM candidate playbooks fixed in advance. Each is measured for bounded reward on nn independent tasks that were not used to produce these candidates. By Hoeffding’s inequality and the union bound, with probability at least 1−δ1-\delta, all candidates satisfy:

|Ĵ(B)−J(B)|≤ϵ=log⁡(2M/δ)2n.|\widehat J(B)-J(B)|\le \epsilon=\sqrt{\frac{\log(2M/\delta)}{2n}}.

Hence the playbook chosen by average score has a true reward at most 2ϵ2\epsilon below the best playbook in this candidate set. Conversely, reducing each playbook’s error to ϵ\epsilon requires:

n≥log⁡(2M/δ)2ϵ2.n\ge\frac{\log(2M/\delta)}{2\epsilon^2}.

This is a comparison guarantee for fixed candidates and independent evaluation. When validation scores are read repeatedly and new playbooks are generated from them, the candidates depend on those tasks, and a new confirmation set or an analysis covering adaptive selection is required.

Longer text can express more candidates. The capacity constraint links expressive power to the difficulty of estimation, but the bound above is usually very conservative, and in practice the relation between sample size and stability must still be plotted.

Finite states do not imply monotone improvement

Combining the playbook and the updater’s state, and fixing the task distribution and the update rule, gives a random process that can be analyzed. Even with only two playbooks, an update that always switches to the other one oscillates indefinitely.

A proof of convergence also requires a description of the transition structure; a proof of convergence to a high-reward state further requires a description of how the selection mechanism relates to the objective. Candidate validation, retaining the current best version and independent confirmation each contribute, and each must correspond to an explicit assumption.

Measuring entry contributions through interventions

For entry eie_i, compare the full playbook with the playbook from which that entry has been removed:

Δi=J(B)−J(B\{ei}).\Delta_i=J(B)-J(B\setminus\{e_i\}).

Replacing the removed part with a placebo of the same length reduces confounding from the change in length. Entries interact, so Δi\Delta_i depends on the current playbook, and the sum of the individual entries’ scores does not give the gain of the whole playbook.

A claim about which entry was used is an observable indication. Randomly disabling entries, replacing their content or ablating groups of entries tests the proposed cause directly.

Exercise

Suppose there are eight candidate playbooks fixed in advance, with rewards between zero and one. Taking δ=0.05\delta=0.05, compute the number of tasks needed for each playbook’s error to be at most 0.1, and compare this with testing each on only six questions. Explain why small trial runs are useful for detecting design problems but insufficient to establish a small gain.

Then construct an example in which two playbook entries depend on each other, and explain why single-entry ablations cannot simply be added. The packaged theory_checks.py recomputes the sample size and the two-state oscillation.

Common errors and corrections

Writing down the supremum and claiming that the algorithm attains it. Report the feasible space, the search method and the empirical reward achieved separately.

Treating many samples of the same question as many independent tasks. Cluster by task, and separate generalization across tasks from randomness within a task.

Inferring from one useful entry that every entry in the playbook contributes. Ablate candidate entries and account for their interactions.

For theoretical background, start from the definitions of policy and return in Sutton and Barto’s Reinforcement Learning, then compare with how Reflexion and GEPA actually perform updates.
