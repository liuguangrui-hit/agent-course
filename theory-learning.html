6.1 经验学习与策略表示

课后深入，约 75 分钟。

一条经验能改变行为，就成为了策略的一部分。但经验究竟学到什么，需要从策略类、目标与更新规则分别说明。

固定原点，定义可行经验

令 ℬK,L\mathcal B_{K,L} 是至多 KK 条、总长不超过 LL 的经验集合。原点 𝒜v\mathcal A_v 固定到一个可观测版本，给出策略类：

Πv={πB:B∈ℬK,L},πB(y∣x)=𝒜v(y∣x,B).\Pi_v=\{\pi_B:B\in\mathcal B_{K,L}\},\qquad \pi_B(y\mid x)=\mathcal A_v(y\mid x,B).

单步任务分布为 DD，回报有界且方向固定，例如 r∈[0,1]r\in[0,1] 越大越好。目标是：

Jv(B)=𝔼x∼D,y∼πB[r(x,y)],B*∈arg⁡maxBJv(B).J_v(B)=\mathbb E_{x\sim D,\,y\sim\pi_B}[r(x,y)],\qquad B^*\in\arg\max_B J_v(B).

原点的无经验表现是 Jv(⌀)J_v(\varnothing)。经验带来的增量在同一任务分布与执行预算下比较。榜的容量约束给出一个明确搜索空间，也让不同方法有可比的输入成本。

更新规则还需要评价与选择

Bt+1=Uϕ(Bt,xt,yt,rt).B_{t+1}=U_\phi(B_t,x_t,y_t,r_t).

UϕU_\phi 可以提议新增条目，再用验证任务比较候选；也可以先从失败中提炼多个做法，分别试验后选择。是否使用梯度，不决定它能否构成回报驱动的策略学习。重要的是反馈怎样影响更新，以及目标是否指向未来回报。

为检验这一点，训练一个使用打乱回报的更新器作对照。回报在训练任务之间打乱，不能只在最后算分时打乱。两者在同一正常评估集上的差异，才反映正确反馈的作用。

单步与序贯的区别

论文回放中，一份材料给一次预测，作答不改变下一篇材料，适合先建情境老虎机模型。研究执行则有状态 sts_t、动作 ata_t 与转移 PP：

Jv(B)=𝔼πB,P[∑t=0T−1γtrt].J_v(B)=\mathbb E_{\pi_B,P}\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^t r_t\right].

动作可能是选一个实验，实验结果改变已知证据，再决定下一个实验。迟来的回报需要分给前面的选择。可以用预算内的最终判别正确率为主目标，再把运行成功与成本作单独指标，避免系统只学会挑容易完成的任务。

只从最终成功轨迹抽经验，会漏掉因选择不当而早早失败的轨迹。训练记录应包含失败与终止原因。

有限候选集能给出什么保证

先考虑事前固定的 MM 张候选榜。每张榜在 nn 个独立任务上测有界回报，任务未用于产生这组候选。由 Hoeffding 不等式与并合界，概率至少 1−δ1-\delta 时，所有候选都满足：

|Ĵ(B)−J(B)|≤ϵ=log⁡(2M/δ)2n.|\widehat J(B)-J(B)|\le \epsilon=\sqrt{\frac{\log(2M/\delta)}{2n}}.

因此按平均分选出的榜，与这组候选中最优榜的真实回报差至多 2ϵ2\epsilon。反过来，要把每张榜的误差压到 ϵ\epsilon，需要：

n≥log⁡(2M/δ)2ϵ2.n\ge\frac{\log(2M/\delta)}{2\epsilon^2}.

这是固定候选、独立评估下的比较保证。不断读取验证成绩再生成新榜时，候选依赖了这些任务，需要新的确认集或覆盖自适应选择的分析。

文本越长，可表达的候选越多。容量约束把表达能力和估计难度接在一起，但上述界通常很保守，实际仍要画样本量与稳定性的关系。

有限状态不推出单调改进

把经验榜和更新器状态合在一起，固定任务分布与更新规则后，可以分析它形成的随机过程。即使只有两张榜，更新每次都切换到另一张，也会来回振荡。

要证明收敛，还需说明转移结构；要证明收敛到高回报状态，还需说明选择机制与目标的关系。候选验证、保留当前最好版本和独立确认各有作用，任何一项都要对应明确假设。

条目信用分配与消融

对条目 eie_i，可以比较完整榜与移除该条目的榜：

Δi=J(B)−J(B\{ei}).\Delta_i=J(B)-J(B\setminus\{e_i\}).

用同长度安慰剂替换被移除部分，可减少篇幅变化带来的混淆。多个条目会相互作用，因此 Δi\Delta_i 依赖当前榜，不能把每条分数简单相加当成全榜收益。

声明使用了哪条经验是可观察线索。随机关闭条目、替换条目内容或做成组消融，才是在改变原因。

练习

设有八张事前候选榜，回报在零到一之间。取 δ=0.05\delta=0.05，算出每张榜误差不超过 0.1 所需的任务量，再与每张只测六题比较。解释为什么小试跑适合发现设计问题，却还不足以判定小幅收益。

再设计一个两条经验互相依赖的例子，说明单条消融为什么不能直接加总。包内 theory_checks.py 会复算样本量与两状态振荡。

常见错误与处理方法

写出上确界，就说算法达到上界。 分别报告可行空间、搜索方法和达到的经验回报。

把同题多次采样当成很多独立任务。 按任务聚类，区分任务间泛化与任务内随机性。

发现一条经验有用，就认为整榜每条都贡献。 对候选条目做消融，保留交互作用。

理论背景可从 Sutton 与 Barto 的 Reinforcement Learning的策略与回报定义读起，再对照 Reflexion 和 GEPA 的实际更新方式。

6.1 Learning through experience

After-class deep dive, about 75 minutes.

Once a playbook entry can change behavior, it has become part of the policy. To explain what is learned, define the policy class, objective and update rule.

Fix the origin and define the allowable playbooks

Let ℬK,L\mathcal B_{K,L} be the set of playbooks with at most KK entries and total length at most LL. Fixing the origin 𝒜v\mathcal A_v to an observable version gives the policy class:

Πv={πB:B∈ℬK,L},πB(y∣x)=𝒜v(y∣x,B).\Pi_v=\{\pi_B:B\in\mathcal B_{K,L}\},\qquad \pi_B(y\mid x)=\mathcal A_v(y\mid x,B).

The single-step task distribution is DD, and the reward is bounded with a fixed direction, for example r∈[0,1]r\in[0,1], higher is better. The objective is:

Jv(B)=𝔼x∼D,y∼πB[r(x,y)],B*∈arg⁡maxBJv(B).J_v(B)=\mathbb E_{x\sim D,\,y\sim\pi_B}[r(x,y)],\qquad B^*\in\arg\max_B J_v(B).

The origin’s performance without experience is Jv(⌀)J_v(\varnothing). The increment from experience is compared under the same task distribution and execution budget. The playbook’s capacity constraint gives an explicit search space, and also gives different methods comparable input costs.

The update rule also needs evaluation and selection

Bt+1=Uϕ(Bt,xt,yt,rt).B_{t+1}=U_\phi(B_t,x_t,y_t,r_t).

UϕU_\phi can propose new entries and then compare candidates on validation tasks; it can also first distill several methods from failures, try each, and then choose. Whether it uses gradients does not decide whether it can be reward-driven policy learning. What matters is how feedback affects the update, and whether the objective concerns future reward.

To test this, train an updater that uses shuffled rewards as a control. Rewards are shuffled across training tasks, not only when scores are computed at the end. Compare the two on the same unshuffled evaluation set to assess the role of correct feedback.

Single-step versus sequential

In paper replay, each piece of material gets one prediction and the answer does not change the next paper, so a contextual bandit is a useful initial model. Research execution has a state sts_t, an action ata_t and a transition PP:

Jv(B)=𝔼πB,P[∑t=0T−1γtrt].J_v(B)=\mathbb E_{\pi_B,P}\left[\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\gamma^t r_t\right].

An action may be choosing an experiment; the experimental result changes the known evidence, which then decides the next experiment. A delayed reward has to be assigned to earlier choices. You can use the rate of correct final discrimination within budget as the main objective, and treat run success and cost as separate metrics, so the system does not just learn to pick tasks that are easy to complete.

Distilling experience only from trajectories that finally succeeded misses trajectories that failed early because of poor choices. Training records should include failures and reasons for termination.

What a finite candidate set can guarantee

First consider MM candidate playbooks fixed in advance. Each is measured for bounded reward on nn independent tasks that were not used to produce these candidates. By Hoeffding’s inequality and the union bound, with probability at least 1−δ1-\delta, all candidates satisfy:

|Ĵ(B)−J(B)|≤ϵ=log⁡(2M/δ)2n.|\widehat J(B)-J(B)|\le \epsilon=\sqrt{\frac{\log(2M/\delta)}{2n}}.

So the playbook chosen by average score has a true reward at most 2ϵ2\epsilon below the best playbook in this candidate set. Conversely, to bring each playbook’s error down to ϵ\epsilon you need:

n≥log⁡(2M/δ)2ϵ2.n\ge\frac{\log(2M/\delta)}{2\epsilon^2}.

This is a comparison guarantee for fixed candidates and independent evaluation. When you keep reading validation scores and generating new playbooks, the candidates depend on those tasks, and you need a new confirmation set or an analysis that covers adaptive selection.

The longer the text, the more candidates it can express. The capacity constraint links expressive power to the difficulty of estimation, but the bound above is usually very conservative, and in practice you still need to plot the relation between sample size and stability.

Finite states do not imply monotone improvement

Combine the playbook and the updater’s state, fix the task distribution and the update rule, and you can analyze the random process they form. Even with only two playbooks, an update that switches to the other one every time oscillates back and forth.

To prove convergence you also need to describe the transition structure; to prove convergence to a high-reward state you also need to describe how the selection mechanism relates to the objective. Candidate validation, keeping the current best version and independent confirmation each play a part, and each must correspond to an explicit assumption.

Measure entry contributions through interventions

For entry eie_i, compare the full playbook with the playbook with that entry removed:

Δi=J(B)−J(B\{ei}).\Delta_i=J(B)-J(B\setminus\{e_i\}).

Replacing the removed part with a placebo of the same length reduces confounding from the change in length. Entries interact, so Δi\Delta_i depends on the current playbook, and adding up each entry’s score does not give the gain of the whole playbook.

A claim about which entry was used is an observable clue. Randomly disabling entries, replacing their content or ablating groups of entries directly tests the proposed cause.

Exercise

Suppose there are eight candidate playbooks fixed in advance, with rewards between zero and one. Taking δ=0.05\delta=0.05, work out the number of tasks needed for each playbook’s error to be at most 0.1, then compare with testing each on only six questions. Explain why small trial runs are good for finding design problems but not enough to establish a small gain.

Then design an example where two playbook entries depend on each other, and explain why single-entry ablations cannot simply be added up. The packaged theory_checks.py recomputes the sample size and the two-state oscillation.

Common mistakes and how to fix them

Writing down the supremum and saying the algorithm reaches the bound. Report the feasible space, the search method and the empirical reward achieved separately.

Treating many samples of the same question as many independent tasks. Cluster by task, and separate generalization across tasks from randomness within a task.

Finding one entry useful and assuming every entry on the playbook contributes. Ablate candidate entries and account for their interactions.

For theoretical background, start from the definitions of policy and return in Sutton and Barto’s Reinforcement Learning, then compare with how Reflexion and GEPA actually update.
