3 实验分析

本节课目标

完成本节课堂训练及课后练习后，学生应能：

  1. 针对一个研究问题提出竞争解释，写出各自的可区分预测，并选择能够区分它们的对照。
  2. 在执行前冻结预测、主要指标、实验单位与分析方案，并识别实际执行与计划之间的偏离。
  3. 为每次运行维护代码提交、数据哈希、参数、环境与执行身份，区分管线检验与正式运行。
  4. 解释配对差值及其区间，区分差异不显著与等价，并说明支持等价所需的事前界和设计。
  5. 依据实验证据更新研究判断，同时保留原始预测以供核查。

课堂用已有的完整预览结果练习分析，用新增对照的短运行检验管线；新增对照的正式推断须等课后完整运行后完成。

课前完成准备，课堂 60 分钟，课后完成复习、独立实践与迁移评价，另可选做深入专题。

课前准备 · 课堂教学 · 课后复习与扩展

本节以一次预测检验为分析材料。研究问题是：验证集同样带有标签噪声时，早停是否失效。配套实验原先预测带噪验证会使 CE 推迟停止并降低准确率，结果四条预测中有三条错误。这一失败引出了更有价值的问题：验证标签中的哪些信息足以支持模型选择。

本节从预览结果出发，先判断哪项实验能进一步检验解释。课堂只运行一条短路径，完整种子和扩展对照在课后完成。

课堂教学

一次失败预测的分析，5 分钟

先核对 preflight.md 中的执行端与环境状态，再分析一次已经完成的预测检验。

预览结果中，四成标签噪声下 CE 的带噪验证早停准确率为 93.25%，干净验证早停为 92.26%，不早停为 68.37%。五个种子配对后，带噪减干净的差值为 0.99 个百分点，95% 区间为负 0.34 到 2.32 个百分点。

这些数字回答了当前比较，但仍留下两种解释：带噪标签仍保留有用的排序信息，或只要提前停止就足以保住大部分表现。需要增加随机停止时点和随机验证标签等对照，才能进一步区分。

判别标准与实验单位，10 分钟

一个研究问题对应两种解释，每种解释给出可测量的预测。选定主要比较后，固定数据切分和分析方式，写明哪项结果支持哪种解释，以及结果无法区分时还需补充什么。

同一个种子应尽量共享数据划分、标签翻转和初始化，使比较集中于干预本身。实验单位是一次独立训练；同一条训练曲线上的多个轮次只是重复记录，不能当作增加种子数。

代码完成后先做冒烟测试，检查输出结构和数值，再提交正式版本。预测、代码和结果分别留有版本，三者的先后关系才完整。

练习一：制定判别计划，10 分钟

读 questions.md 和 preview/ 中的真实结果。
围绕带噪验证集为什么有效，写两个竞争解释。
先选一个最小对照，固定主要指标与比较方向，写出有实际意义的效应大小。
把种子，数据划分，早停规则和分析方法写入 prediction.md，再提交。
说明什么结果能区分两种解释，什么结果仍需继续实验。

练习二：执行核查与运行扩展，20 分钟

先核查预览的提交字段与审查记录，再为本次运行建立真实提交和独立审查记录。
根据计划改实验代码，加入 --seeds、--epochs 和输出目录参数。
每次运行保存代码提交和数据哈希，同时记录参数、环境与随机种子。
先做单种子短运行，核对预期行数和有限值，并确认验证集与测试集分离，结果写 smoke/。
通过后提交正式代码，把完整任务的后台命令和停止方法准备好。课堂用短任务检验后台入口，正式任务课后提交。返回任务标识、日志与完成检查入口。

正式训练的早停以验证交叉熵为监测指标，耐心值为 8，并恢复最好一轮的权重。沿用预览实现时，应先核对这一规则；第 2 课问题页还给出了按验证准确率选择轮次的另一种设计，两种设计应分别登记。

课堂可以在现有实现上做短运行，再用完整预览练习分析。完整运行和新增对照都列入课后计划，短运行的数字不能写入正式结论。

练习三：区间解读、管线核查与审阅，10 分钟

分析演练用 preview/ 的五个种子，重算带噪减干净的配对差、均值与区间，写到 smoke/analysis-rehearsal.md。
注明演练沿用的旧比较，只回答带噪与干净验证的差异，不拿它回答本次新增对照。
核对新对照两臂已有的同种子短运行，检查配对键与输出字段，只验证分析管线。
本轮 prediction.md 保持冻结；正式 result.md 等课后完整运行再写。
另一家 Agent 只读演练文件、冒烟记录和 prediction.md，检查数据归属与计划是否可检验，意见及主张检查写到 smoke/review.md。

预测命中只是一次检验的结果。研究理解是否改进，还要看它能否继续预测新条件下的结果。预测失败后，在知识库中更新相应的解释与下一个问题，同时保留当时的预测原件。

课堂检查与本节小结，5 分钟

常见错误与处理方法

预测正确即宣称机制得到证明。 写出竞争解释，补充能使两种解释给出不同预测的对照。

测试集参与轮次选择。 选择规则只读取验证信息。测试指标仅用于最终报告，相关代码入口分别核查。

任务从进程列表中消失即视为运行完成。 同时检查退出码、预期产物和内容完整性，失败的运行也保留状态。

差异不显著即写成效果相同。 报告配对差值及其区间。若要主张等价，须先确定等价界，再设计相应检验。

本节小结

实验结果现在具有完整的执行记录：代码提交与数据版本对应，后台任务有日志与退出状态，结论有事前计划和独立核查。无论在哪台机器上运行，后续接手者都能找到同一项比较。

课后复习与扩展

概念复习、正反例分析及能力达成评价中的独立实践与迁移任务为本节必做内容；其余深入专题按研究需要选做。

概念复习，15 分钟

不看课堂记录，写出两种竞争解释，并各写一个会使自己改变判断的结果。再核对 prediction.md 和运行身份，说明主要指标、实验单位与停止规则。把计划与实际执行之间的偏差写入 review-after-class.md，预测原件保持不变。

正反例辨析，20 分钟

以下数字仅用于辨析。甲报告配对差为 0.3 个百分点，95% 区间为负 1.2 到 1.8，并据此认为两种做法等价。乙报告同一组数字，指出区间仍包含事先关心的差异，提出按事前等价界补充有判别力的实验。判断哪一种表述得到当前结果的支持，并写出支持等价所需的界和实验设计。另说明：若把同一种子的五个轮次当作五次独立实验，会改变哪一个分母。

先写出判据，再查看参考思路

当前区间只说明这批数据对差值的约束，不能据此推出等价。等价需要先给出有实际意义的界，再看相应的检验与区间是否满足该界。轮次共享同一次训练，不能替代独立种子。把它们当作独立重复，会虚增区间公式中的 n 和自由度，使标准误被人为缩小；需要更多证据时，应增加独立重复和有效对照。

扩展学习

先补完课堂上的冒烟测试与计划审阅，再按冻结计划完成全部种子和对照。完整结果到齐后，按相同种子配对，写出正式的 result.md 回答主要问题，再交另一家 Agent 核查并写入 review.md。完整训练提交后台后，可先做推导与分析练习，任务结束后再汇总；训练等待时间不计为学习活动。

3 Experimental analysis

Learning outcomes

After completing this session and its review and extension work, students should be able to:

  1. Formulate competing explanations for an observed result and derive predictions that distinguish them.
  2. Design an effective control and a pre-specified analysis plan, including the experimental unit, pairing and decision criteria.
  3. Maintain the identity of code, data, environment and execution for every run, so that each result can be traced to what produced it.
  4. Interpret paired differences and their uncertainty intervals, distinguishing lack of evidence for a difference from evidence of equivalence.
  5. Update a research judgment in light of the evidence, including predictions that turn out to be wrong.

The outcomes are assessed at three levels. In class, students freeze a prediction, modify the experimental code, check the pipeline with short runs and rehearse the analysis on existing complete results. After class, they complete the full run under the frozen plan and draw the formal inference. The extension paths require the same reasoning on a further control or on the student’s own research question.

Preparation before class, a 60-minute class session, then required review, independent work and transfer assessment, with optional further study.

Preparation · Class session · Review and extension

An experiment is informative when its possible outcomes would change a research judgment. This session therefore begins from competing explanations, fixes the prediction and analysis before execution, and treats the record of code, data and environment as part of the evidence.

The case material comes from the companion experiment on noisy labels. Its question was whether early stopping still works when the validation set is also noisy. The experiment originally predicted that noisy validation would make CE stop later and lose accuracy, and three of its four predictions were wrong. The failed predictions led to a better question: which information in the validation labels is sufficient to guide model selection?

The session starts from the preview results and asks which experiment would distinguish the explanations. In class, students carry out short runs that check the pipeline; the full set of seeds and the extended controls, on which the formal conclusion rests, are run after class.

Class session

A prediction that turned out wrong, 5 minutes

Check the executor and environment status in preflight.md, then examine a prediction test that has already been completed.

In the preview, with 40% label noise, CE’s accuracy is 93.25% with early stopping on noisy validation, 92.26% with early stopping on clean validation, and 68.37% without early stopping. Paired over five seeds, noisy minus clean is 0.99 percentage points, with a 95% interval from minus 0.34 to 2.32 percentage points.

These numbers answer the current comparison but leave two explanations open: noisy labels retain useful ranking information, or stopping early is in itself enough to retain most of the performance. Further controls, such as a random stopping point and random validation labels, are needed to decide between them.

Defining the decision criteria before the experiment, 10 minutes

A question comes with two explanations, each giving a measurable prediction. Once the main comparison is chosen, the data split and the analysis are fixed. The plan states which result supports which explanation, and what will be added if they cannot be distinguished.

Runs with the same seed should share data splits, label flips and initialization wherever possible, so that the comparison isolates the intervention. The experimental unit is one independent training run; recording many epochs of the same training curve does not add seeds.

After the code is written, a smoke test is run first to check the output structure and values, and the version for full execution is then committed. The prediction, the code and the results each keep their own version, so that their order can be demonstrated.

Exercise 1: planning an experiment that distinguishes explanations, 10 minutes

Read questions.md and the real results in preview/.
Write two competing explanations for why a noisy validation set works.
First choose one minimal control, fix the main metric and the direction of comparison, and write down an effect size that matters in practice.
Write the seeds, data split, early-stopping rule and analysis method into prediction.md, then commit it.
State which result would separate the two explanations, and which result would still need further experiments.

Exercise 2: checking execution before scaling up, 20 minutes

First check the commit fields and review record of the preview, then create a real commit and an independent review record for this run.
Change the experiment code according to the plan, adding the --seeds and --epochs parameters and an output directory parameter.
Each run saves the code commit, data hash, parameters, environment and random seed.
First do a short single-seed run and check the expected number of rows, finite values, and that validation and test are separate. Write results to smoke/.
Once it passes, commit the code for the full run and prepare the background command for the full job and the way to stop it. In class, use a short job to test the background entry; the full job is submitted after class. Return the job identifier, the log and the entry for checking completion.

Early stopping in full training uses validation cross-entropy with patience 8 and restores the weights of the best epoch. When reusing the preview, check this rule first; the Lesson 2 question page also describes a design that selects by validation accuracy, and the two designs are registered separately.

In class, short runs can be made on the existing implementation, and the analysis is rehearsed on the full preview. The full run and the new controls form part of the after-class plan, and numbers from short runs are not used in the final conclusions.

Exercise 3: interpreting the interval, checking the pipeline and handing over for review, 10 minutes

For the analysis rehearsal, use the five seeds in preview/ to recompute the paired noisy-minus-clean difference, its mean and interval, and write them to smoke/analysis-rehearsal.md.
Note that the rehearsal reuses the old comparison and answers only the difference between noisy and clean validation; it is not used to answer this round's new control.
Check the pairing keys and output fields in the existing matched-seed short runs of both arms of the new control. This verifies only the analysis pipeline.
Keep this round's prediction.md frozen; the final result.md is written after the full after-class run.
The other vendor's agent reads only the rehearsal file, the smoke record and prediction.md, checks where the data belong and whether the plan can be tested, and writes its comments and a claim check to smoke/review.md.

A correct prediction is a single test result. An improved explanation should also make useful predictions under new conditions. After a wrong prediction, the explanation and the next question are written back into the knowledge base, and the original prediction is kept.

Common errors and summary, 5 minutes

Common errors and corrections

Concluding that a mechanism is proven because the prediction was correct. State the competing explanation and add the control under which the two explanations predict different outcomes.

Using the test set to choose the epoch. The selection rule reads only validation information. Test metrics are reserved for the final report, and the code paths involved are checked separately.

Assuming that a job which has disappeared has finished. Check the exit code, the expected products and their completeness together, and record a status for failures as well.

Reporting two methods as equally good because the difference is not significant. Report the paired difference and its interval. A claim of equivalence requires an equivalence margin set in advance and a corresponding test.

Method summary

Results now have traceable run records: each identifies the code commit and data, background jobs have logs and exit statuses, and conclusions refer to a plan recorded in advance and to an independent check. Whichever machine executes the run, the next person to take over can locate the same comparison.

Review and extension

Conceptual review, analysis of contrasting examples, and the independent and transfer tasks specified in Assessment are required for this session. Other extension topics are optional, except for the required readings identified below.

Unaided review, 15 minutes

Without consulting the class record, write the two competing explanations and, for each, outline one result that would change your judgment. Then check prediction.md and the run identity, and state the main metric, the experimental unit and the stopping rule. Record any deviations between the plan and what was actually run in review-after-class.md, keeping the original prediction.

Analysis of contrasting examples, 20 minutes

The following numbers are for discussion only. Statement A reports a paired difference of 0.3 percentage points with a 95% interval from minus 1.2 to 1.8, and concludes that the two approaches are equivalent. Statement B reports the same numbers, notes that the interval still contains the difference of interest set in advance, and proposes a more discriminating experiment based on an equivalence margin set in advance. Judge which statement the current result supports, then specify the margin and design needed to support equivalence. Also identify which denominator changes if the five epochs of one seed are treated as five independent experiments.

Record your criteria before reading the commentary

The current interval shows only how this batch of data constrains the difference; it cannot yet establish equivalence. Equivalence first requires a margin with practical meaning, followed by a check of whether the corresponding test and interval satisfy it. Epochs share one training run and cannot replace independent seeds. Treating them as independent replications inflates the n and degrees of freedom in the interval formula and artificially shrinks the standard error; where more support is needed, add independent replications and effective controls.

Further study

First complete the class smoke test and plan review, then run the remaining seeds and controls according to the frozen plan. When the full results are available, pair them by seed, write the final result.md to answer the main question, and have the other vendor’s agent check it in review.md. While the full training runs in the background, work on the derivation and analysis exercises, and aggregate once the job finishes; training time is separate from the time allocated to the exercises.

Assessment

Attainment of the outcomes is judged from the reasoning that connects plan, execution and conclusion. Students should be able to explain why the chosen control distinguishes the two explanations; show from the run records which code, data and environment produced each result; justify why the class short runs verify only the pipeline while the formal inference rests on the complete after-class run; and interpret the paired interval without treating a non-significant difference as equivalence. Transfer is assessed by applying the same design to a further control or to the student’s own question, and stating in advance which result would change the current explanation.
