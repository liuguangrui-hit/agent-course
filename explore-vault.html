2.2 知识库搭建

学习目标

  1. 以带稳定标识的观察记录证据，区分观察、作者解释与研究者解释，并识别证据之间的依赖关系。
  2. 按实验条件对相互冲突的结果分层，把无法统一的分歧转化为判别实验设计。
  3. 在加入反例后沿依赖关系确定受影响的概念与问题，评价知识更新是否落实到研究决策。

课后深入专题，约 55 分钟。

一篇新论文可能推翻原有的理解。此时需要回答：知识库中哪些页面应当随之改变？若答案只是再增加一篇笔记，这个知识库仍然只是阅读档案。

以观察为基本单位

论文页中的每条结果都有稳定标识。内容写明研究对象与干预、比较项与指标，并注明出处。作者的解释与结果分开记录，研究者自己的解释另列一项。

E01
对象与条件：数据集，模型，训练与选择规则
干预与比较：具体改变了什么，其余怎样一致
观察：指标与数值，重复单位和不确定性
出处：文件版本，PDF 顺序页，表号或段落
解释：作者怎样解释，证据支持到哪一步

同一结果在三个段落中出现，仍然只是一条证据。三篇论文使用同一个数据集时，也要记录这种依赖关系，避免把相关证据当作三次独立重复。

概念页的综合与更新

概念页开头用几句话陈述当前认识，其后列出支持证据和反例，并链接到论文页的证据标识。读者应能看出每段综合依赖哪些观察。

多篇结果表面相反时，按条件分层：噪声模型、训练预算与选择规则是否相同。无法统一时保留两种解释，并写出一个能区分它们的问题。

新增论文进入后，先列它改变了概念页哪句话。
同方向的新证据合入对应位置，条件不同的补条件差异，反方向的进入分歧表。
更新开头的综合，使它覆盖现有证据。保留修改原因与来源。

由分歧到实验设计

示例库中的排序反转涉及多处训练设置。知识库先记录两项观察，再提出两种竞争解释：优化日程改变了方法的行为，或模型选择方式造成了表面差异。

据此可以构造二维实验设计：一个维度改变优化日程，另一个维度改变选择轮次的规则，其余设置保持一致。若反转只随其中一个维度变化，下一步就围绕该维度继续检验。

这一步使知识库从材料集合成为研究依据。每个新问题都应说明它要消除哪一处不确定性。

反例与依赖更新

为一条证据设置新的状态，例如待复核或已被新版本替代。沿链接列出受影响的概念页和问题。先处理核心主张直接依赖的部分，再处理展示页面。

可以由 Agent 生成受影响清单，但共享综述由一位合并者更新。已完成的实验保留当时的问题与预测，后来出现的反例只追加更新说明，不改写历史记录。

把 E01 标为待复核，找出引用它的概念和问题。
逐项判断：结论仍有其他独立证据、需要补一项实验，或当前无法继续使用。
保存影响清单，再按清单更新，不改原件与旧实验预测。

目录结构

vault/
  sources/       原件及文件清单
  papers/        带证据标识的论文页
  concepts/      当前综合与分歧
  questions.md   带来源的问题
  updates/       影响清单和更新原因

Obsidian 的双向链接适合浏览，普通 Markdown 链接适合稳定交付。文件移动后检查引用，证据标识保持不变。关系图用于发现孤立材料，节点数量多并不说明认识更完整。

练习

选择概念页中的一条重要判断，为它加入一篇结论相反的论文。提交三个文件：新证据页、更新后的概念页、受影响问题清单。请同学只看清单，检查能否据此指出应当优先补做的实验。

检查标准

综合段落发生了有依据的变化；旧证据仍可追溯；新增解释注明为推测；问题清单指出了补证办法。四项均满足，更新才落实到研究决策。

常见错误与处理方法

把推测写成论文发现。 用独立字段分别记录观察、作者解释与研究者解释。

每篇新论文都追加在概念页末尾。 先说明它改变了哪句话，再重写综合，来源链接留在对应位置。

只更新笔记，未更新依赖它的问题。 维护受影响清单，按主张的依赖关系追溯到实验入口。

Obsidian 官方帮助中的双向链接与反向链接功能，可用于查找哪些页面引用了某条证据。

2.2 Building a knowledge base

Learning outcomes

After this section, students should be able to:

  1. Record observations as stable evidence units that separate conditions, intervention, result, source and interpretation, and that account for dependence between studies.
  2. Maintain a synthesis that reflects supporting and contrary evidence, and design a factorial experiment that distinguishes competing explanations of a disagreement.
  3. Propagate the effect of new or contrary evidence through dependent conclusions and questions while preserving the historical record.

Extension reading and exercises, about 55 minutes.

A knowledge base is tested when a new paper contradicts an earlier understanding. If the only response it allows is to add another note, it is still a reading archive. This section develops a structure in which the affected pages can be identified and updated.

Starting from a single observation

Each result on a paper page receives a stable identifier. Its content states the object of study, the intervention, the comparison, the metric and the source. The authors’ explanation is kept apart from the result, and the student’s own explanation is listed separately again.

E01
Object and conditions: dataset, model, training and selection rules
Intervention and comparison: what exactly changed, and how everything else was kept the same
Observation: metric and value, unit of replication and uncertainty
Source: file version, PDF page in sequence, table number or paragraph
Explanation: how the authors explain it, and how far the evidence supports it

A result that appears in three paragraphs is still one piece of evidence. When three papers reuse the same dataset, that dependence is recorded as well, so that related evidence is not counted as three independent replications.

Concept pages record the current understanding

A concept page opens with a few sentences stating the current understanding, followed by supporting evidence and counterexamples, linked to the evidence identifiers on paper pages. Readers should be able to see which observations the synthesis depends on.

When several results appear to point in opposite directions, stratify them by condition: whether the noise model, training budget and selection rule are the same. When the results cannot be reconciled, both explanations are kept and a question that can distinguish them is written.

When a new paper comes in, first list which sentence of the concept page it changes.
New evidence in the same direction goes in the corresponding place; evidence under different conditions adds a condition difference; evidence in the opposite direction goes into the disagreement table.
Update the opening synthesis so it covers the existing evidence. Keep the reason for the change and its source.

From disagreement to experiment

The ranking reversal in the sample knowledge base involves several training settings. The knowledge base first records the two observations and then states competing explanations: the optimization schedule changes how the methods behave, or the method of model selection creates an apparent difference.

A two-factor design addresses this: vary the optimization schedule along one axis and the epoch-selection rule along the other, keeping all other settings fixed. If the reversal appears only when one factor changes, the next experiment continues along that factor.

This step turns the knowledge base from a collection of material into a basis for research. Every new question can identify the uncertainty it is intended to resolve.

Propagating updates from counterexamples

A piece of evidence can be given a new status, such as pending recheck or superseded by a new version. Following the links, list the affected concept pages and questions. Evidence on which core claims depend directly is addressed first, then the display pages.

The agent can generate the list of affected items, but a single editor updates the shared synthesis. Completed experiments keep the question and prediction as they stood at the time; later counterexamples add an update note and do not rewrite history.

Mark E01 as pending recheck and find the concepts and questions that cite it.
Judge each: the conclusion still has other independent evidence, it needs one more experiment, or it cannot be used for now.
Save the impact list, then update according to the list, without changing originals or old experiment predictions.

A simple folder structure

vault/
  sources/       originals and the file list
  papers/        paper pages with evidence identifiers
  concepts/      current synthesis and disagreements
  questions.md   questions with their sources
  updates/       impact lists and reasons for updates

Obsidian’s wikilinks are convenient for browsing; plain Markdown links are more stable for delivery. After moving files, check references and keep evidence identifiers stable. The graph view helps locate isolated material; a larger number of nodes does not in itself indicate a more complete understanding.

Exercise

Select one important judgment on a concept page and add a paper pointing in the opposite direction. Submit three files: the new evidence page, the updated concept page and the list of affected questions. Ask a colleague to read only the list and judge whether it shows which experiment should be added first.

Assessment criteria

The synthesis changed for a stated reason; the old evidence can still be traced; new explanations are marked as speculation; and the question list shows how evidence will be added. All four are required for the update to inform research decisions.

Common errors and corrections

Recording speculation as a paper’s finding. Use separate fields for the observation, the authors’ explanation and the student’s own explanation.

Appending each new paper to the end of the concept page. First state which sentence it changes, then rewrite the synthesis with source links in the appropriate places.

Updating notes without updating the questions that depend on them. Maintain the list of affected items and follow claim dependencies through to the experimental plan.

Wikilinks and backlinks, described in Obsidian’s official help, can be used to find what cites a given piece of evidence.
