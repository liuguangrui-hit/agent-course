2.2 知识库搭建

课后深入，约 55 分钟。

一篇新论文推翻了你原来的理解。知识库里哪些页面应该变化？若答案只是再加一篇笔记，它还只是阅读档案。

从一条观察开始

论文页中的每条结果给一个稳定标识。内容写清研究对象与干预、比较项与指标，并注明出处。作者的解释与结果分开，自己的解释再单列。

E01
对象与条件：数据集，模型，训练与选择规则
干预与比较：具体改变了什么，其余怎样一致
观察：指标与数值，重复单位和不确定性
出处：文件版本，PDF 顺序页，表号或段落
解释：作者怎样解释，证据支持到哪一步

同一个结果在三个段落里出现，仍是一条证据。三篇论文复用同一个数据集，也要记录依赖关系，避免把相关证据当成三次独立重复。

概念页的综合与更新

概念页开头用几句话说当前认识，后面给支撑和反例，链接到论文页的证据标识。读者应当看得出这段综合依赖哪些观察。

当多篇结果表面相反，按条件分层：噪声模型、训练预算与选择规则是否相同。无法统一时保留两种解释，再写一个能区分它们的问题。

新增论文进入后，先列它改变了概念页哪句话。
同方向的新证据合入对应位置，条件不同的补条件差异，反方向的进入分歧表。
更新开头的综合，使它覆盖现有证据。保留修改原因与来源。

分歧怎样变成实验

示例库的排序反转涉及多处训练设置。知识库先记录两个观察，再提出竞争解释：优化日程改变方法行为，或模型选择方式造成表面差别。

实验可以形成一个二维设计。横向改优化日程，纵向改选轮次规则，其他设置保持一致。若只在一个轴变化时反转，下一步就围绕这个轴继续检验。

这一步把知识库从材料集合变成研究依据。每个新问题都应说明它要解决哪一处不确定性。

反例与依赖更新

给一条证据加入新状态，例如待复核或被新版本替代。沿链接列出受影响的概念页和问题。先处理核心主张直接依赖的部分，再处理展示页。

可以让 Agent 生成受影响清单，但由一个合并者更新共享综述。已经完成的实验保留当时问题与预测，后来的反例只追加更新说明，历史不重写。

把 E01 标为待复核，找出引用它的概念和问题。
逐项判断：结论仍有其他独立证据、需要补一项实验，或当前无法继续使用。
保存影响清单，再按清单更新，不改原件与旧实验预测。

文件夹保持简单

vault/
  sources/       原件及文件清单
  papers/        带证据标识的论文页
  concepts/      当前综合与分歧
  questions.md   带来源的问题
  updates/       影响清单和更新原因

Obsidian 的双链适合浏览，普通 Markdown 链接适合稳定交付。文件移动后查引用，证据标识保持稳定。关系图用来发现孤立材料，不能单凭节点多就判断认识更完整。

练习

选概念页中的一条重要判断，给它添加一篇反方向论文。交出三个文件：新证据页，更新后的概念页，受影响问题清单。请同学只看清单，检查是否能指出哪项实验应当先补。

检查标准

综合段发生了有理由的变化；旧证据仍能追溯；新增解释写明是推测；问题清单能指出补证办法。四项都完成，更新才落实到研究决策。

常见错误与处理方法

把推测写成论文发现。 用独立字段记录观察、作者解释与自己的解释。

每篇新论文都追加到概念页末尾。 先说它改变哪句话，再重写综合，来源链接留在对应位置。

只更新笔记，没有更新依赖它的问题。 维护受影响清单，按主张依赖查到实验入口。

Obsidian 官方帮助中的双链与反向链接，可用来查找谁正在引用一条证据。

2.2 Building a knowledge base

After-class deep dive, about 55 minutes.

A new paper overturns your earlier understanding. Which pages in the knowledge base should change? If the answer is only “add one more note”, it is still just a reading archive.

Start from one observation

Give each result on a paper page a stable identifier. Its content states the object of study, the intervention, the comparison, the metric and the source. The authors’ explanation is kept apart from the result, and your own explanation is listed separately again.

E01
Object and conditions: dataset, model, training and selection rules
Intervention and comparison: what exactly changed, and how everything else was kept the same
Observation: metric and value, unit of replication and uncertainty
Source: file version, PDF page in sequence, table number or paragraph
Explanation: how the authors explain it, and how far the evidence supports it

A result that appears in three paragraphs is still one piece of evidence. When three papers reuse the same dataset, record that dependence as well, so related evidence is not counted as three independent replications.

Concept pages hold the current understanding

A concept page opens with a few sentences on the current understanding, followed by support and counterexamples, linked to the evidence identifiers on paper pages. Readers should be able to see which observations the synthesis depends on.

When several results appear to point in opposite directions, stratify by condition: are the noise model, training budget and selection rule the same? When they cannot be reconciled, keep both explanations, then write a question that can tell them apart.

When a new paper comes in, first list which sentence of the concept page it changes.
New evidence in the same direction goes in the corresponding place; evidence under different conditions adds a condition difference; evidence in the opposite direction goes into the disagreement table.
Update the opening synthesis so it covers the existing evidence. Keep the reason for the change and its source.

How a disagreement becomes an experiment

The ranking reversal in the sample knowledge base involves several training settings. The knowledge base first records the two observations, then proposes competing explanations: the optimization schedule changes how the methods behave, or the way models are selected creates an apparent difference.

Use a two-factor design: vary the optimization schedule along one axis and the epoch-selection rule along the other, keeping all other settings fixed. If the reversal appears only when one axis changes, the next step keeps testing along that axis.

This step turns the knowledge base from a collection of material into a basis for research. Every new question can point to the uncertainty it is resolving.

Propagate updates from counterexamples

Give a piece of evidence a new status, such as pending recheck or superseded by a new version. Following the links, list the affected concept pages and questions. First address the evidence on which core claims depend directly, then the display pages.

You can let the agent generate the list of affected items, but one editor updates the shared synthesis. Completed experiments keep the question and prediction of the time; later counterexamples only append an update note, and history is not rewritten.

Mark E01 as pending recheck and find the concepts and questions that cite it.
Judge each: the conclusion still has other independent evidence, it needs one more experiment, or it cannot be used for now.
Save the impact list, then update according to the list, without changing originals or old experiment predictions.

Keep the folders simple

vault/
  sources/       originals and the file list
  papers/        paper pages with evidence identifiers
  concepts/      current synthesis and disagreements
  questions.md   questions with their sources
  updates/       impact lists and reasons for updates

Obsidian’s wikilinks are good for browsing; plain Markdown links are good for stable delivery. After moving files, check references, and keep evidence identifiers stable. Use the graph view to find isolated material; more nodes alone do not mean the understanding is more complete.

Exercise

Pick one important judgment on a concept page and add a paper pointing the other way. Hand in three files: the new evidence page, the updated concept page and the list of affected questions. Have a classmate look only at the list and check whether it shows which experiment should be added first.

Checking criteria

The synthesis changed for a stated reason; old evidence can still be traced; new explanations are marked as speculation; the question list shows how to add evidence. All four are needed for the update to inform research decisions.

Common mistakes and how to fix them

Writing speculation as a paper’s finding. Use separate fields for the observation, the authors’ explanation and your own explanation.

Appending every new paper to the end of the concept page. First say which sentence it changes, then rewrite the synthesis, with source links in the right places.

Updating the notes but not the questions that depend on them. Maintain the list of affected items and follow claim dependencies through to the experimental plan.

Wikilinks and backlinks in Obsidian’s official help can be used to find what is citing a piece of evidence.
