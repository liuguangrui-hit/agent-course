1.6 文献引用与刊会投稿

学习目标

  1. 区分引用的身份核查与含义核查，分别用结构化元数据和原文支持位置作出判断。
  2. 依据目标刊会的当前要求核查实际 PDF，包括版式、字体、元数据与匿名性。
  3. 在篇幅受限时按叙事作用决定内容取舍，并保持模板要求不变。

课后深入专题，约 30 分钟。

引用可以检索到，还要核查它是否支持正文中的说法。投稿 PDF 编译成功，还要核查它是否符合目标刊会的交付要求。本节介绍成稿后的这两类检查。

文献引用

为每条参考文献保存 DOI 或 arXiv 标识，核对标题、作者与发表信息，再确认正文使用的是哪个版本。正式版与预印本的图表可能不同，笔记中的页码须对应所用版本。

读取参考文献，逐条到出版社或书目接口核对。
输出 exact，mismatch，unresolved 三类，保留查询 URL 和时间。
题目检索返回的近似结果只作候选，进一步用作者和标识确认。
为正文实际引用的版本保留记录。

Crossref、DBLP 和 arXiv的覆盖范围各不相同。某个接口没有记录时，继续核对出版方与作者的公开页面；仍无法确定的，保留为待核。

引用关系的核查

从正文中抽出依赖引用的主张，整理为主张与出处对照表。在原文中找到支持段落，记录证据类型：实验结果、理论推导或作者讨论。综述转引的发现须追溯到原始研究后再判断。

对正文每个带引用的结论，给出原文支持位置和证据类型。
检查是否把作者的推测写成已证实结果，是否把一种设置的结果换成另一种设置。
缺支撑时先找直接证据，找不到就列出需要补的原文证据或实验。

身份核查依据结构化元数据，含义核查依据全文。两者的通过状态分别保存。

刊会投稿

确定目标届次，保存官方作者指南和模板入口，按当前要求检查页数、匿名要求与附件。需要压缩篇幅时，按叙事作用决定取舍：删除重复解释，移出次要实验，保留核心证据。

图在首页的位置、字体要求和附录安排，均以目标模板及同类正式论文为准。照搬其他刊会的惯例，容易使 Agent 朝错误方向修改。

实际 PDF 的检查

完整构建本版 PDF，汇总未定义引用和超出版心等日志。
读取字体与元数据，按目标刊会要求检查。
逐页看渲染结果，检查图注位置、表格边界和链接。
如需双盲，检查作者栏和致谢，再查链接、图内路径与 PDF 元数据。

代码仓库和补充材料同样可能含有身份信息。需要匿名发布时，先把拟公开的文件打包，在本地核查全部内容，再由用户确认提交。

练习

复制练习稿，把其中一条引用的年份改错，再把一条真实引用移到它无法支持的句子之后。分别运行身份核查与含义核查，观察两处问题各由哪一项检查发现。

参考判断

错误年份应在元数据核对时被发现。第二处引用的文献身份仍然真实，需要通过原文支持位置发现。两项检查分开进行，才能解释为何一条引用通过了其中一项检查，却仍不能使用。

常见错误与处理方法

题目相似即判为同一篇文献。 进一步核对作者、标识和版本，区分候选匹配与最终匹配。

为满足页数限制而缩小模板。 按叙事作用排列删减建议，修改正文内容，保持模板要求不变。

匿名检查只覆盖正文。 同时检查 PDF 属性、图内文字与附件链接，按目标要求交付。

1.6 Citations and submission

Learning outcomes

After this section, students should be able to:

  1. Verify the identity and version of each reference against structured metadata, distinguishing candidate matches from confirmed ones.
  2. Assess whether a cited source supports the specific claim made in the text, and classify the type of evidence it provides.
  3. Check a compiled manuscript and its supplementary materials against a venue’s current submission and anonymity requirements.

Extension reading and exercises, about 30 minutes.

Locating a reference does not establish that it supports the text, and a PDF that compiles may still fail the target venue’s submission requirements. This section covers both checks once the draft is complete.

Verifying reference details

For every reference, keep its DOI or arXiv identifier, check the title, authors and publication details, and confirm which version the text uses. Figures and tables may differ between the published version and the preprint, and page numbers in notes refer to a specific version.

Read the references and check each one against the publisher or a bibliographic API.
Output three classes: exact, mismatch, unresolved, keeping the query URL and time.
Approximate matches from a title search are only candidates; confirm further with authors and identifiers.
Keep a record of the version actually cited in the text.

Crossref, DBLP and arXiv cover different ground. When one API has no record, continue with the publisher’s and authors’ public pages; if the result remains uncertain, record it as pending.

Checking what each citation supports

Extract the claims in the text that rely on citations and tabulate claims against sources. Locate the supporting passage in the original and record whether the evidence is an experimental result, a theoretical derivation or the authors’ discussion. Findings cited through a review are traced to the original study before being judged.

For every cited conclusion in the text, give the supporting location in the original and the type of evidence.
Check whether an author's speculation has been written as an established result, and whether a result from one setting has been swapped for another setting.
Where support is missing, first look for direct evidence; if none is found, list the evidence from the original or the experiment that needs to be added.

Structured metadata are used to check reference details, and full texts to check what a citation supports. The two outcomes are recorded separately.

Checking the venue’s submission requirements

Identify the target conference year or journal issue and save the official author guidelines and template links. Check page count, anonymity and supplementary files against the current requirements. When reducing length, consider each passage’s role in the argument: remove repeated explanations, move secondary experiments, and keep the core evidence.

The position of figures on the first page, font requirements and appendix arrangements follow the target template and published papers of the same type. Conventions carried over from elsewhere can lead the agent to make corrections in the wrong direction.

Checking the actual PDF

Build this version's PDF in full and collect log entries such as undefined references and content outside the text block.
Read the fonts and metadata and check them against the target venue's requirements.
Look at the rendered result page by page, checking caption placement, table borders and links.
If double-blind review is required, check the author block, acknowledgments, links, paths inside figures and PDF metadata.

Code repositories and supplementary materials also carry identifying information. When anonymous release is required, package the files to be made public first, check their full content locally, and then have the user confirm the submission.

Exercise

Copy the exercise draft, change the year of one citation to an incorrect one, and move another genuine citation to a sentence it cannot support. Run the identity check and the support check separately and record which check detects each problem.

Suggested analysis

The incorrect year should be detected by the metadata check. In the second case the reference is still genuine, so detecting the error requires checking the purported supporting passage in the source. Only when the two checks are kept separate can one explain why a citation passed one of them and still cannot be used.

Common errors and corrections

Treating two papers as the same because their titles are similar. Match authors, identifiers and versions as well, and keep candidates distinct from the final match.

Shrinking the template to meet the page limit. Rank cuts by their role in the argument, revise the content and keep the template’s requirements.

Checking anonymity in the body text only. Also check PDF properties, text inside figures and attachment links, and deliver as the venue requires.
