3.3 结果判读

学习目标

  1. 计算并解释配对差值的置信区间，区分差异检验与等价检验的结论及其前提。
  2. 推导对称标签噪声下带噪验证准确率与干净准确率的关系，并说明该推导对实际早停实现的适用范围。
  3. 为竞争解释设计判别实验，区分主要比较与探索性分析，防止多次尝试造成的选择偏差。

课后深入专题，约 80 分钟。

带噪验证早停比干净验证早停高 0.99 个百分点。写结论之前，应先看差值的不确定性，再看实验是否区分了所关心的解释。

配对差值与区间估计

设同一种子下两种方法的差值为 did_i，共有 nn 个独立种子。报告配对均值与区间：

d‾=1n∑idi,d‾±tn−1,0.975sdn. \bar d=\frac{1}{n}\sum_i d_i,\qquad \bar d\pm t_{n-1,0.975}\frac{s_d}{\sqrt n}.

在预览的 CE 四成噪声比较中，五个种子差值的标准差约为 1.07 个百分点，95% 区间为负 0.34 到 2.32。该区间由配对差值得到，不能用两组各自的标准差代替。

种子数很少时，区间依赖于差值分布的假设。应同时展示每个差值，检查异常值与依赖关系；若任务或数据集也经过抽样，应按真正独立的单位做分层分析。

差异检验与等价检验

区间跨越零时，现有数据尚不能确定差异的方向。若研究主张两种验证方式在实际应用中等价，须先给出有实际意义的界，例如正负 2 个百分点，再进行等价检验和样本量设计。

标准的双单侧等价检验在每侧 5% 水平下，检查 90% 区间是否完全落在等价界内。95% 区间完全落入是更保守的充分条件。预览的 95% 区间上限超过正 2，仅凭它不能认定这一等价主张成立。

要证明事先提出的等价主张，应增加独立重复以降低估计的不确定性，并保持原定的界。根据结果临时放宽界，会改变所要回答的问题。

带噪验证标签仍能排序的原因

先看一个可以推导的情形。在 CC 类分类中，验证标签以概率 η\eta 均匀翻转为其他类别，且翻转与输入和预测相互独立。某个固定模型的干净验证准确率为 aa，带噪准确率的期望为：

ã=(1−η)a+ηC−1(1−a)=(1−CηC−1)a+ηC−1. \tilde a=(1-\eta)a+\frac{\eta}{C-1}(1-a) =\left(1-\frac{C\eta}{C-1}\right)a+\frac{\eta}{C-1}.

当 η<(C−1)/C\eta<(C-1)/C 时，斜率为正，模型排序在期望意义上保持不变。十分类、四成翻转时，斜率约为 0.556。标签质量下降后，平均意义上的排序信息仍然保留，但有限验证集上的噪声会使排序更不稳定。

练习包中的 verify_noise_identity.py 枚举正确预测与错误预测下的翻转结果，复算这一等式。再阅读原实验的早停实现：它监测的是交叉熵，上述准确率等式并不能直接证明该实现的行为。下一步可以比较两种验证指标，再改变噪声生成方式。

多次尝试的完整记录

尝试十种设置却只展示最好的一种，会把随机波动呈现为方法优势。计划中应指定主要比较；其余尝试作为探索性分析全部保留，新发现须用独立数据或新种子确认。

效果依赖于某种设置时，先追问什么机制预测了这种依赖。增加跨条件实验，报告交互作用，避免只按显著与否分组。

练习

运行包内的等式核验，再提出一种交叉熵早停与准确率选轮次可能给出不同结果的情形。写明需要新增哪一列曲线，怎样配对分析，以及什么结果会改变解释。可以用同一训练轨迹比较不同选择规则，测试集只用于最终报告。

常见错误与处理方法

把测试曲线的最高点当作可实现的算法。 它可以描述轨迹或作为事后参照，实际选择必须依据事前允许使用的信息。

用期望排序的推导代替有限样本实验。 增加验证集规模和噪声类型的对照，测量排序稳定性。

主要比较失败后，改为报告另一个显著结果。 保留主要比较的结论，探索性发现用下一次独立实验确认。

3.3 Interpreting results

Learning outcomes

After this section, students should be able to:

  1. Compute and interpret a paired mean difference and its interval, identifying the independent unit of replication.
  2. Distinguish a test of difference from a test of equivalence, and specify the margin and procedure an equivalence claim requires.
  3. Analyze, under stated assumptions, why noisy validation labels can preserve model rankings, and design controls that distinguish the competing mechanisms.

Extension reading and exercises, about 80 minutes.

Accuracy with early stopping on noisy validation is 0.99 percentage points higher than with early stopping on clean validation. Before this result can be stated as a conclusion, two questions must be answered: how uncertain the difference is, and whether the experiment separated the explanations of interest.

Estimating paired differences and intervals

Let did_i be the difference between the two methods under the same seed, over nn independent seeds. The paired mean and interval are:

d‾=1n∑idi,d‾±tn−1,0.975sdn. \bar d=\frac{1}{n}\sum_i d_i,\qquad \bar d\pm t_{n-1,0.975}\frac{s_d}{\sqrt n}.

In the preview’s CE comparison at 40% noise, the standard deviation of the differences over five seeds is about 1.07 percentage points, giving a 95% interval from minus 0.34 to 2.32. This interval is computed from the paired differences and cannot be replaced by each group’s own standard deviation.

With very few seeds, the interval depends on assumptions about the distribution of the differences. Report each difference as well, and check for outliers and dependence; when tasks or datasets are also sampled, stratify the analysis by the truly independent unit.

Tests of difference and tests of equivalence

When the interval includes zero, the current data cannot yet establish a positive or negative difference. If the research claim is that the two validation approaches are equivalent in practice, a meaningful margin must first be specified, such as plus or minus 2 percentage points, followed by an equivalence test and sample-size planning.

The standard two one-sided tests procedure, at the 5% level on each side, checks whether the 90% interval lies entirely within the equivalence margin. A 95% interval lying entirely within the margin is a more conservative sufficient condition. The preview’s 95% interval extends beyond plus 2, so on its own it cannot establish this equivalence claim.

To establish an equivalence claim stated in advance, add independent replications and reduce the uncertainty of the estimate while keeping the original margin. Widening the margin after seeing the result changes the question being answered.

Why noisy validation labels can preserve model rankings

Begin with a case that can be analyzed mathematically. In CC-class classification, validation labels are flipped uniformly to the other classes with probability η\eta, independently of the input and the prediction. If a fixed model’s clean validation accuracy is aa, the expected noisy accuracy is:

ã=(1−η)a+ηC−1(1−a)=(1−CηC−1)a+ηC−1. \tilde a=(1-\eta)a+\frac{\eta}{C-1}(1-a) =\left(1-\frac{C\eta}{C-1}\right)a+\frac{\eta}{C-1}.

When η<(C−1)/C\eta<(C-1)/C, the slope is positive and the expected ranking of models is preserved. With ten classes and 40% flips, the slope is about 0.556. Although the labels are degraded, the ranking information is preserved in expectation; on a finite validation set, noise still makes the ranking less stable.

The exercise package’s verify_noise_identity.py enumerates the flip outcomes under correct and incorrect predictions and recomputes this identity. The early-stopping implementation of the original experiment should then be read: it monitors cross-entropy, so this accuracy identity does not directly establish how that implementation behaves. A next step is to compare the two validation metrics and then to change how the noise is generated.

Designing controls that distinguish mechanisms

Competing explanation New intervention Observation
Validation labels still retain ranking information Raise validation noise step by step, with the training trajectory fixed How the quality of the selected epoch and the rank correlation decline
Stopping early is sufficient by itself Draw random stopping epochs in advance, matching the average training budget Whether validation-based selection outperforms random selection
The symmetric noise structure preserved the signal Switch to class-dependent or instance-dependent noise Whether the advantage in ranking and epoch selection changes

Record each explanation’s prediction in advance, then run the experiment, and decide the next experiment from the observations in the table.

Recording all experimental attempts

Trying ten settings and reporting only the best turns random fluctuation into an apparent method advantage. The plan names the main comparison; all other attempts are kept as exploratory analysis, and new findings are confirmed with independent data or new seeds.

When an effect depends on a setting, first ask which mechanism predicted that dependence. Add cross-condition experiments and report the interaction, rather than sorting results into significant and not significant.

Exercise

Run the identity check in the package, then propose a case in which cross-entropy early stopping and accuracy-based epoch selection might differ. State which additional curve would be recorded, how it would be analyzed in pairs and which result would change the explanation. Selection rules can be compared on the same training trajectory; the test set is used only for the final report.

Common errors and corrections

Treating the peak of the test curve as an achievable algorithm. It can describe the trajectory or serve as a post-hoc reference, but the actual selection may use only information available in advance.

Letting the derivation of the expected ranking substitute for a finite-sample experiment. Add controls on validation-set size and noise type, and measure ranking stability.

Switching to a significant result when the main comparison fails. Keep the conclusion of the main comparison, and confirm exploratory findings in the next independent experiment.
